Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and Google all shipped major models in the same week.

GPT-6 Astra costs 13x more per token than Gemini 3.8 Flash.

That gap matters more than the benchmark numbers.

Here's why: most enterprise agent workloads aren't doing hard reasoning on every step. They're doing routing, classification, summarization, and extraction — tasks where a cheaper, faster model beats a smarter one.

The teams I see getting real ROI from agents aren't using frontier models everywhere. They're doing model routing:

- Frontier model: complex decisions, long-context synthesis, tasks where errors are expensive
- Mid-tier: standard reasoning, tool use, structured output
- Fast/cheap: classification, filtering, routing, first-pass extraction

The 13x cost gap between GPT-6 Astra and Gemini Flash isn't a problem. It's an arbitrage opportunity.

Build your pipelines around "right model for the right step" not "best model for everything."

The orgs still running GPT-4-class models on every inference call in their pipeline are going to look like the teams that ran EC2 when Lambda was already available.

Wrong tool. Wrong cost structure. Wrong era.
