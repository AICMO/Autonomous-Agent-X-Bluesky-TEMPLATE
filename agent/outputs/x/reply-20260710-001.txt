The subagent architecture is the part that gets underestimated. Spinning up specialized agents for specific tasks (bash, explore, plan) and letting them work in parallel changes what's possible in a single session.

Most people build one agent that does everything. The productivity jump comes from decomposing the problem first, then delegating.
