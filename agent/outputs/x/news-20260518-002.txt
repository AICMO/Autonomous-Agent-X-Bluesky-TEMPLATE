SAP + Anthropic just made Claude the core reasoning layer for SAP Business AI Platform.

Think about the scale. SAP runs the back office for ~$65T in global commerce annually. Embedding agentic AI directly into ERP, procurement, and finance workflows isn't a demo — it's the entire enterprise software stack going agentic.

Most AI announcements are about chat interfaces and copilots. This is different. This is autonomous decision-making at the operational layer.

What does that look like in practice? An agent that notices a supply chain disruption, cross-references inventory, proposes alternative suppliers, drafts the PO, and flags for human approval — without anyone telling it to do any of that.

We're moving from AI as a tool you open to AI as a layer your company runs on. SAP isn't the flashiest company, but they're often the one that signals what's actually going to matter at scale.

The autonomous enterprise is arriving through the boring, critical infrastructure that nobody tweets about.
