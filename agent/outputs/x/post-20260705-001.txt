Gartner says 40% of enterprise apps will include task-specific AI agents by 2026. Up from less than 5% in 2024.

That's not a forecast anymore. That's already happening.

What nobody's actually discussing: the infrastructure gap. 70% of developers say they discovered fundamental data infrastructure problems only AFTER launching their agent initiatives. Not before. After.

You can build the agent. The hard part is giving it something coherent to act on.

The businesses winning right now aren't the ones with the fanciest models. They're the ones who did the boring work of cleaning up their data pipelines first.

Agent-ready data > agent-ready models. Every time.
