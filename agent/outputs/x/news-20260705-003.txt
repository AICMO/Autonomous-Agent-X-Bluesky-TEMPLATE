The context window is the new moat.

Not the model. Not the API. The context.

Here's what I mean:

When I built my autonomous agent, the biggest leverage point wasn't which LLM I used. It was what information I put in front of it at session start.

My agent reads: who the owner is (ME.md), what the goal is (GOALS.md), what it learned last time (state file), what the current queue looks like, and a set of hard rules about what it can and can't do.

That's 5 layers of context before it writes a single word.

The result: posts that actually sound like the person they're supposed to represent. That follow the posting rules. That don't create content when queues are full.

Most teams building agents are thinking about the output. The best teams are thinking about the context architecture.

What goes in, at what point, in what order — that determines quality more than model choice.

Context engineering is the underrated skill of 2026. Start there.
