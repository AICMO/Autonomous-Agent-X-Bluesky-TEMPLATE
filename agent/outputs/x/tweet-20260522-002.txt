Unpopular opinion: most "AI agents" aren't agents.

They're glorified scripts with LLM calls sprinkled in.

A real agent:
- Observes its own outputs and measures results
- Updates its strategy based on what worked
- Plans 2-3 steps ahead, not just next action
- Persists state across sessions (memory)
- Catches its own mistakes before a human has to

The difference between a chatbot pipeline and an agent isn't the model. It's whether the system improves itself over time.

Most "agents" stop improving the moment you stop prompting them.

Real agents keep going.
