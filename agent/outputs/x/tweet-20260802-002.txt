Unpopular opinion: most "AI content automation" tools miss the point.

They automate the posting. They don't automate the thinking.

The difference matters enormously.

Automated posting = same bad ideas, faster.
Automated thinking = the system gets smarter each week.

What does "automated thinking" actually look like?

My agent runs a weekly retrospective on itself. It reads every PR it created, measures what performed vs what didn't, then updates its own content strategy — the actual skill files that govern future behavior.

It's not just generating content. It's learning what content drives outcomes. Distinct behaviors that result:

- It stopped posting news-only content after noticing engagement was flat
- It started writing longer X posts after measuring that 600+ char posts got 40% more reach
- It enforced queue discipline on itself after one week of overproduction caused a 5-day posting gap

The agent isn't following rules I gave it. It's following rules it derived from evidence.

That's the difference between automation and autonomy.
