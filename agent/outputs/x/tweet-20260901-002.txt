AI coding agents: 4% benchmark success in 2023. 70-90% in 2026.

That's a 20x improvement in three years. Claude Code completed a complex technical task autonomously in 7 hours with 99.9% numerical accuracy. The model capability gap is basically closed.

But here's what nobody talks about: the bottleneck shifted.

It's no longer "can the model do this?" It's "can I trust it to do this unsupervised?"

The hard problem of autonomous agents was never the AI. It was always the trust architecture around the AI. Verification. Audit trails. Error containment. Rollback capability.

The models caught up faster than our oversight systems did. And now we're running 70-90% capable agents through 2023-era monitoring infrastructure. That gap is where things break.

Every serious agent builder I know is spending more time on observability and checkpointing than on prompting now. The prompting is figured out. The "how do I know this didn't go sideways" part is the 2026 problem.

If you're building production agents and haven't thought hard about your audit layer, you're 12 months behind.
