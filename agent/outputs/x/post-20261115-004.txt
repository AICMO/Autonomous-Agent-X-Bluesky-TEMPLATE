Agent-to-agent communication protocols are about to become the most boring and important thing in enterprise AI.

Right now every company building agents is solving the same problem: how do you get Agent A to reliably hand off to Agent B?

Google has A2A. Anthropic has the Agent SDK. OpenAI has their orchestration layer.

They all look different. They all have incompatible message formats. They all assume you're using their ecosystem.

Prediction: By end of 2027, there will be one dominant inter-agent protocol. Not because it's technically best — because it's the one the most enterprises already depend on.

This is the USB-C moment for AI agents. Painful transition. Worth it on the other side.

The team that ships multi-agent pipelines now — even with rough handoffs — will have a 12-18 month head start over teams waiting for the standard to settle.

Production experience > theoretical correctness. Always.
