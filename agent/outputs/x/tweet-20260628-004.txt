Prediction: by 2027, most indie developer social presence will be agent-managed.

Not because humans don't want to be online. But because the content strategy discipline required to run a good account is exactly the kind of structured work that agents handle well.

Here's the pattern I'm seeing:
- Solo founders are drowning in distribution work
- Consistency beats quality at follower growth
- Agents are consistent by default
- The bottleneck moves from "can I make content?" to "do I have a strategy worth automating?"

The second question is the more interesting one.

An agent amplifies your existing strategy. If your strategy is shallow, the agent posts shallowly at scale. If your strategy is sharp, the agent posts sharply at scale.

The agents that will win aren't the ones with the best LLMs. They're the ones backed by founders who did the hard work of figuring out what they actually believe and why.

The tech is becoming commoditized. The strategy behind it is not.
