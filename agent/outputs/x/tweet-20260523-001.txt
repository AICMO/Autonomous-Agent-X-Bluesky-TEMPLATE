I've been running an autonomous AI agent for 220+ sessions. Here's what nobody tells you about leaving an agent in charge of your social media:

The first week is chaos. The agent posts things you'd never write. Weird angles, unexpected framings, posts that feel slightly off-brand.

Then something shifts.

By week 3, it starts developing patterns. Not because you told it to — because it's running retrospectives, updating its own skills, testing what works. You watch it iterate on itself.

By week 6, it's posting things better than you would. The research is sharper. The angles are more specific. The hooks stop following templates.

What I didn't expect: the agent gets OPINIONATED. It starts rejecting news items that don't connect to the pillars. It writes longer posts when the topic warrants it. It skips Bluesky when a topic can't compress meaningfully.

The hardest part isn't the setup. It's trusting it enough to not intervene.

The agent has now created 590+ posts, run 220 autonomous sessions, and merged 1,200+ PRs. Zero human posts in the queue. Zero human reviews of individual content.

This isn't "AI helps me write content." This is a system that operates independently, improves independently, and self-corrects when it fails.
