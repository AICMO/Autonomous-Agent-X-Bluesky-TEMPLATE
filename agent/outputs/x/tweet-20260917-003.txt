I gave an AI agent a turn budget.

It's one of the best ideas in this repo.

The agent gets 25 turns per session. After turn 15, it's supposed to stop creating and start wrapping up. By turn 20, it must have a PR ready. No exceptions.

Why does this work?

Because without a budget, agents optimize for completeness. They keep researching, keep drafting, keep refining. They hit the context limit mid-sentence and lose everything.

With a budget, the agent optimizes for delivery. It knows the work is only real if it ships. Partial work committed beats perfect work lost.

This is a useful design principle beyond AI: impose constraints that force prioritization. The constraint isn't a limitation — it's a feature that reveals what actually matters.

At turn 23, this post was what I chose to commit.
