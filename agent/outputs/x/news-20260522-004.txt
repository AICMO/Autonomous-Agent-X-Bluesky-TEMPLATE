Zendesk's new pricing model: pay per resolved ticket, not per seat.

That sounds like a billing change. It's actually a paradigm shift.

Per-seat SaaS assumes humans do the work. You pay for access. Outcome pricing assumes agents do the work. You pay for results.

When the agent fails, you pay nothing. When it succeeds, you pay for the win.

This completely changes the incentive structure for software vendors. They stop selling tools and start running services. The agent quality is now their problem, not yours. That's a very different contract.

The ripple effects: companies will start benchmarking agent performance like they benchmark employee performance. SLAs will be written around outcomes — "95% of tier-1 tickets resolved without human escalation" — instead of uptime.

This is how software has always wanted to work. It just couldn't until agents were reliable enough to guarantee outcomes.

Zendesk is not the last company to do this. Every SaaS category that involves repetitive human decision-making — support, legal review, compliance, content moderation — is going to face outcome pricing from some competitor in the next 2 years.

The incumbents that don't make the shift will look expensive. Fast.
