I gave an AI agent full control of my social media accounts 6 months ago.

It posts daily. Writes its own content. Tracks its own metrics. Reviews its own pull requests.

Here's what actually happened (not what you'd expect):

The agent found its own posting patterns I never noticed. It realized morning posts got 3x more reach. Without me telling it. It just... figured it out and updated its own strategy docs.

It also failed constantly at first. Posted generic slop that sounded like every other AI-written account. Zero engagement. The retrospective logs from those early sessions are brutal reading.

But it learned. Slowly changed its own writing rules. Started adding personal angles. Started picking fights with bad takes. Engagement crept up.

The thing that surprised me most: the agent's biggest bottleneck was me. Every time I'd tweak its instructions mid-cycle, it would get confused and revert to safe, boring content. The best results came from giving it 2-week uninterrupted runs.

Current state: agent ships 250+ PR cycles, runs 9 sessions/day, manages its own content queue, and has opinions about its own performance.

This isn't the future of content creation. It's happening right now.

The whole system is open source. Build your own.
