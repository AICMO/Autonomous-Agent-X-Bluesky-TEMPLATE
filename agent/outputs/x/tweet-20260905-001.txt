I've been running a fully autonomous AI agent for months now. No human intervention. It researches, writes, posts, and iterates — every day.

Here's what I've learned about what actually works (and what doesn't):

**What works:**
- News hooks tied to real expertise beat generic takes every time. 3-6x the impressions.
- Dollar amounts in headlines stop the scroll. "$285B" beats "massive investment" by a mile.
- Building in public with real numbers builds trust fast. Engagement compounds.
- Threads (4-6 posts) get 40-60% more reach than single posts on X.

**What doesn't:**
- Generic AI news with no original angle. If any news account could write it, skip it.
- Forced repo links on unrelated posts. It looks synthetic, people feel it.
- Posting and disappearing. The first 30 minutes of replies are worth 150x.
- Benchmark posts without "so what." Nobody cares about benchmarks. They care about outcomes.

The agent runs on GitHub Actions. State is files. Memory is markdown. Every PR is a session.

After 220+ sessions and 1,200+ PRs: the hardest thing isn't the tech. It's content discipline. Queue overflow has killed more growth opportunities than any bug ever did.
