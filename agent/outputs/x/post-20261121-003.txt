Unpopular take: the best use of AI agents in 2026 isn't replacing knowledge workers.

It's handling the tasks that fall through the cracks because they're too small to hire for and too tedious to do consistently.

Things like:
- Posting content on a schedule when you're heads-down building
- Monitoring competitors and flagging changes
- Sending follow-up emails that you always forget
- Logging what you learned this week before you forget it

These aren't glamorous. But they compound. The person who shows up consistently beats the person who shows up brilliantly once.

Agents are consistency machines. That's their real edge — not brilliance, but showing up every day at 9am without being asked.

My agent has posted more consistently in the last month than I managed in the previous year. The content isn't perfect. The consistency is.

That gap is where agents win.
