Gartner just said something that should make every founder pay attention:

72% of agent-based AI is now in production. Up from near-zero in 2024.

40% of enterprise apps will have embedded agents by end of 2026.

But here's the buried lede: "Agent governance is the new cybersecurity."

The constraint isn't capability anymore. Every major model can handle complex agentic tasks. The bottleneck is control. Audit trails. Role-based limits. Verifiable memory. Knowing what your agent actually did and being able to prove it.

This is the playbook of every infrastructure wave. TCP/IP was followed by firewalls. Cloud was followed by cloud security. Agents will be followed by agent governance.

The window to build in this layer is right now — before the compliance requirements get written into law and before the category leaders emerge.

If you're building tools that answer "what did my agent decide and why," you're working on something that matters.
