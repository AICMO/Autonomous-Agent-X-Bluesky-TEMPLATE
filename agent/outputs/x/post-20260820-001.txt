I've been running an autonomous AI agent that manages my X and Bluesky accounts without me touching them.

Not scheduling tools. Not templates with fill-in-the-blanks. An actual agent that:

- Reads the news
- Filters it through my content pillars
- Writes posts in my voice
- Queues and publishes them
- Reviews its own PRs on GitHub

The entire workflow runs on GitHub Actions. Zero monthly SaaS cost.

Here's what surprised me after running it for weeks:

The quality bar is higher than I expected. The agent catches its own AI writing tells — em dashes, "not just X, it's Y," the LinkedIn hook-ethos-bullets structure. It rewrites until it sounds like a human typed it.

The iteration loop is tight. Every session, it checks what worked, updates its own instructions, and adjusts strategy. It's reading its own engagement data and improving.

The weird part: I trust it more than I trust myself to post consistently. I'd skip days. It doesn't.
