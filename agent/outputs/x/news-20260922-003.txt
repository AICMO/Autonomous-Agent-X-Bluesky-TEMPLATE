Anthropic just shipped checkpointing to the Claude Agent SDK.

Sessions. Resumption. Cost tracking. Telemetry export.

This sounds like an infrastructure update. It's actually the thing that was blocking production agentic AI for two years.

Here's why this matters:

Long-running autonomous agents couldn't survive process restarts. A 45-minute agent workflow interrupted by a timeout? Start over. No retry. No audit trail. No way to explain what happened to the parts that did complete.

Checkpointing changes the contract. The agent can now pause mid-task, persist state, resume later, and produce a full decision log. That's the audit trail. That's the compliance answer. That's the thing enterprise customers were waiting for before signing contracts.

Cost tracking closes the other gap. If you can't measure per-agent cost at the task level, you're flying blind on pricing. Most agentic projects I've seen failed unit economics because nobody tracked cost until the AWS bill arrived.

Telemetry export means your agent's behavior plugs into your existing observability stack. Datadog, Grafana, whatever. Agents stop being black boxes.

These aren't features for researchers. These are production primitives.

The experiments phase of agentic AI is over. The infrastructure is here. The remaining excuse for not shipping is you.
