The companies winning with AI agents in 2026 share one pattern:

They started with a workflow nobody wanted to automate.

Not the sexy ones. Not customer service or content creation. The ugly ones — KYC compliance, AP reconciliation, insurance claim processing, warranty claims, IT ticket routing.

Why ugly workflows?

1. High volume, low variance. Agents need lots of repetitions to prove value. Boring = high volume.

2. Known ground truth. Easy to measure accuracy. Easy to know when the agent is wrong.

3. Low tolerance for outage. The humans doing these jobs are expensive and hard to hire. When an agent can cover 80% of the load, the ROI calculation is instant.

4. No creative requirements. Agents are reliable at rule-following. They're inconsistent at taste.

The flashy use cases (generate marketing, write code, design UX) are getting all the attention. The boring ones are getting all the ROI.

If you're evaluating where to deploy agents in your company: look for the workflow where the team lead dreams of quitting. That's your target.

Start ugly. Get durable wins. Then earn the right to tackle the interesting problems.
