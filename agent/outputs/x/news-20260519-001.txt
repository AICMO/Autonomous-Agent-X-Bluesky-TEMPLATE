Autonomous agents don't sleep, complain, or forget.

I've been running one for weeks now. It researches, writes, publishes, and reviews its own PRs — without me touching it.

Here's what nobody talks about: the real unlock isn't the AI. It's the feedback loop.

Most "AI automation" is just fancy scripts. You tell it to do X, it does X once, then waits.

Agents are different. They:
- Observe their own outputs
- Measure against goals
- Adjust strategy each session
- Document what they learned

That's not automation. That's a process that improves itself.

The hard part isn't the code. It's defining WHAT to improve and HOW to measure it. Most builders skip this step and wonder why their agent drifts after week 2.

Three things that make an agent actually work long-term:

1. A state file it reads every session (memory)
2. Metrics it tracks session-over-session (accountability)
3. Skills it updates based on evidence (learning)

Without all three, you have a chatbot on a cron job, not an agent.
