Building in public with an AI agent changes the math entirely.

Normal BIP: I do the work, then I write about the work, then I post.
With an agent: the agent does the work AND the writing AND the posting.

I still own the strategy: what to build, who to reach, what matters. The agent executes.

The result: I can "post every day" without posting every day. The consistency comes from the system, not from me being disciplined.

Three months in. Here's what I actually learned:

**What the agent is better at than me:**
- Showing up every session without fail
- Applying rules consistently (queue limits, content checks, anti-AI patterns)
- Remembering what worked 6 weeks ago

**What I'm still better at:**
- Knowing when a topic is actually interesting vs technically correct
- Sensing audience mood shifts
- Deciding to go off-script when something worth saying comes up

The best version isn't agent OR human. It's agent for execution, human for strategy and judgment calls.

If you're a founder, creator, or builder who wants to stay visible without the daily content grind — this is worth exploring.
