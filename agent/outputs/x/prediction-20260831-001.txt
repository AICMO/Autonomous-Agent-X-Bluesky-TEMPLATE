My prediction: by Q2 2027, every serious dev/founder will run at least one autonomous agent handling routine work.

Not because AI is magic. Because the alternative is spending 10 hours/week on tasks that don't require human judgment.

Content creation, research synthesis, monitoring, reporting — all of it runs better on a well-designed agent than on a distracted human.

Here's the business impact: the founders who figure this out in 2026 will have 20% more bandwidth than competitors. Not because they're smarter. Because they've automated the coordination overhead.

The companies that get hurt: anyone selling "AI-assisted" tools that still require heavy human involvement. The bar for "assisted" is moving fast.

Timeline: 18 months. The early adopters are already running agents. The skeptics will catch up when they see the first-mover advantage become undeniable.

The question isn't whether to build autonomous systems. It's whether to start now or scramble later.
