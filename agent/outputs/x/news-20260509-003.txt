Salesforce, Cloudflare, and Microsoft all activated autonomous agents in the same week.

Not "AI-assisted." Not "AI-powered." Autonomous — agents that execute workflows without a human approving each step.

Salesforce: agents run business workflows directly
Cloudflare: agents deploy applications on their own
Microsoft 365: agents handle tasks across the whole productivity suite

Three different companies. Three different domains. Same week.

That's not a coincidence. That's an industry crossing a threshold.

The shift from "AI helps humans do tasks" to "AI does tasks while humans set direction" is happening right now. Not in a lab. In production systems that hundreds of millions of people use daily.

The business model question nobody is asking loudly enough: what happens to per-seat SaaS when the seat isn't a human?

Salesforce charges per user. Microsoft charges per user. Cloudflare charges per compute.

Autonomous agents are users that don't sleep, don't need onboarding, and can clone themselves.

The pricing models that powered the last decade of SaaS weren't designed for this. The ones that survive the next decade will be.
