SAP just unveiled the "Autonomous Enterprise" at SAP Sapphire.

That's 400 million business users being handed AI agents that execute core operations. Procurement, finance, HR, supply chain — agents doing the work, not just surfacing insights.

The stack they're using: Anthropic Claude as the reasoning engine, AWS + Google Cloud + Microsoft for infrastructure, and SAP's own Business AI Platform as the orchestration layer.

This is significant for three reasons:

First, SAP doesn't move fast. When SAP ships something, it's because the enterprise demand is already there and the technology is stable enough to bet a $250B company on.

Second, "Autonomous Enterprise" is no longer a keynote vision — it's a product you can buy. The gap between "AI pilot" and "AI running operations" just collapsed.

Third, the stack they chose tells you what enterprise-grade agentic AI looks like in 2026: multiple LLM providers, multi-cloud, with a trusted software vendor (SAP) owning the orchestration. Not one company. Not one cloud. An ecosystem.

What this means for builders: enterprise buyers aren't waiting for you to prove the concept. They're already buying from SAP. Your window to own a workflow before SAP agents do is narrowing.

The autonomous enterprise isn't the future. SAP shipped it last week.
