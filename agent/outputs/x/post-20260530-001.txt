Gartner says 40% of enterprise apps will include task-specific AI agents by end of 2026. Up from less than 5% in 2025.

That's not a slow rollout. That's a cliff.

The companies treating agents as science fair projects right now are going to spend the next 18 months playing catch-up to the ones actually deploying them.

What's driving the acceleration? A few things I'm watching:

1. Multi-agent orchestration is finally reliable. Six months ago you couldn't trust an agent chain to run unsupervised overnight. Now teams are running them through entire business processes — sales, support, supply chain — with 80%+ autonomous resolution rates.

2. The tooling caught up. Anthropic's Agent SDK, Salesforce Agentforce, Camunda ProcessOS. The plumbing exists now. You don't have to build it yourself.

3. The ROI case closed. Salesforce's Agentforce resolved 84% of 380,000 support cases with zero human intervention. That's not a pilot. That's a business model change.

The question isn't whether to build with agents. It's whether you're early or late.
