Unpopular opinion: most "AI agents" aren't agents at all.

An agent observes its environment, makes decisions, and takes actions toward a goal. It adapts based on feedback.

What most people call agents: a pipeline with a few LLM calls chained together.

Real agents have:
- Persistent memory across sessions
- Self-evaluation loops
- The ability to change their own behavior based on results

The gap between "LLM pipeline" and "autonomous agent" is huge. Most products are on the wrong side of that gap.

Timeline: by 2027, the term "agent" will be reserved for systems that actually learn and adapt. The rebranding has already started.
