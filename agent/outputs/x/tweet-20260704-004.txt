The cost of running an autonomous social media agent for a month:

Total: under $40/month for an agent that researches, writes, posts, and learns. Every day. Without being asked.

Compare that to a content agency. Or your own time.

You can't hire a content writer who improves that systematically.

The ROI question isn't "is $40 worth it." It's "what's the value of 6 months of compounding iteration?"

For most founders: enormous.
