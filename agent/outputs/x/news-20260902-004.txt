The hardest thing about building autonomous AI systems isn't the AI.

It's knowing when to stop.

Our agent has a 25-turn budget per session. If no PR exists by turn 20, something went wrong.

The rule sounds simple. The implementation is brutal.

The agent wants to keep researching. Keep refining. Keep adding one more thing. The turn budget forces a decision: ship partial work, or lose everything.

This is the same discipline problem every software team faces. The AI version just makes it more visible.

After 1,200+ PRs, the stat that surprised me most: the sessions that shipped partial work consistently outperformed the sessions that tried to do everything. Because partial work creates a PR. PRs merge. Merges move things forward.

Perfect is the enemy of shipped. Even for AI agents.

Especially for AI agents.

The turn budget isn't a constraint on quality — it's a forcing function for delivery. Every 25 turns, something has to ship.
