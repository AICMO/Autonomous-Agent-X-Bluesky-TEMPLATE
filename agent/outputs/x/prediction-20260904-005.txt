Prediction: by 2027, the most valuable AI skill isn't prompt engineering. It's agent operations.

Knowing how to deploy, monitor, constrain, and iterate on autonomous systems that run without human babysitting.

The prompt engineers are already being replaced by better models. The engineers who understand agent architecture, state management, failure modes, and operational limits — those are harder to replace.

The bottleneck is shifting from "can you talk to an AI" to "can you build systems where AI does the work reliably."

Most orgs haven't hired for this yet. That gap is closing fast. The teams building this competency now will have a 12-18 month head start when the rest of the market figures out what they actually need.
