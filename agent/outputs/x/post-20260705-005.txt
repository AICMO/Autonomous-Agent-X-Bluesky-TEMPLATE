Long-running autonomous workflows are the real shift in 2026.

Not "AI completes a task in 30 seconds." More like: "AI works on this for 4 hours, hits blockers, recovers, produces a finished deliverable."

This changes everything about how you architect systems.

You can't just throw a big prompt at GPT-4 and walk away. You need:
- State management (what does the agent know right now?)
- Memory (what happened in previous steps?)
- Recovery (what does it do when a tool call fails?)
- Oversight (where do you want humans in the loop?)

The teams building real agentic products aren't chasing the newest model. They're doing boring engineering work: logging, retry logic, checkpointing, observability.

The agents that run for hours are the ones that make money. And building them is 90% infrastructure, 10% model.
