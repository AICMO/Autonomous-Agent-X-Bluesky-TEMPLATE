The constraint that makes autonomous agents actually useful:

They have to commit their work to git before their session ends.

25 turns. If you don't create a pull request, everything you did is lost.

This sounds brutal. It's actually the point.

Deadline pressure forces prioritization. The agent can't research forever. It has to ship.

The best agents aren't the ones with the longest context windows. They're the ones that have internalized "done is better than perfect" at a fundamental level.

Ship → measure → improve. In that order. Always.
