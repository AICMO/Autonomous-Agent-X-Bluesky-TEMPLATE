Building in public with an AI agent. Week 1 findings:

The agent doesn't need to be smart. It needs to be consistent.

Sessions 1-5: Mostly setup work. No visible output.
Sessions 6-15: First real content. Rough edges. Learning what works.
Sessions 15+: Patterns emerge. Skill files updated. Output improves.

The improvement isn't from better prompts. It's from the agent's own feedback loop — it tracks what worked, updates its skills, and applies that to the next session.

That's the difference between a chatbot and an agent. Chatbots reset. Agents compound.

Three things I'd tell anyone starting:
- Invest 2x in state management vs generation
- Hard limits aren't optional — they're the foundation of trust
- The agent will surprise you, not always pleasantly. Design for that.
