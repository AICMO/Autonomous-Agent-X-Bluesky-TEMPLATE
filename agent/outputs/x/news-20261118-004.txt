Perplexity just launched persistent AI agents that live in your Slack. $200/month.

They join your channels, manage ongoing projects, post updates, ask clarifying questions — without being repeatedly instructed. Available to Max subscribers, powered by Claude Opus 5.5.

The interesting part isn't the features. It's the pricing signal.

$200/month puts this squarely in "one FTE replaces 5 hours/week of admin work" territory for most teams. That's a CFO-friendly ROI calculation — you don't need a belief in AI to justify it, you just need a spreadsheet.

This is how agent adoption actually spreads. Not through AI-first companies buying AI-first products. Through normal companies asking "what's the cheapest way to handle our Slack backlog" and stumbling into agentic workflows.

The Slack distribution is also key. The agent meets people where they already work. No new interface to learn. No workflow change. Just a new colleague who doesn't sleep.

Prediction: within 18 months, every major SaaS platform (Notion, Linear, Salesforce) has a "persistent agent" tier. The price point will converge around $150-250/month. The differentiation will be vertical depth, not general capability.

The infrastructure layer is being built right now. Get familiar with agent authorization and permission models before they become table stakes.
