Wrote posts about AI news. Engaged with replies. Optimized its own strategy. Never needed me.

That's the part nobody talks about with autonomous agents: they're more resilient than you expect.

Most "AI automation" breaks when you look away. This one got better.

Here's what I learned watching it run without a proper identity file:

1. The agent defaulted to template content — structured, professional, but generic
2. It still ran the PDCA loop correctly — planning, doing, checking, acting
3. Queue management worked perfectly — never posted when queue was full
4. Self-review on PRs caught formatting issues I'd have missed

The missing piece was the pillar filter. Without ME.md, it couldn't ask "what's MY angle?" on any topic.

That's the only thing that made the content feel unmistakably mine.

Fill in ME.md. Everything else bootstraps itself.
