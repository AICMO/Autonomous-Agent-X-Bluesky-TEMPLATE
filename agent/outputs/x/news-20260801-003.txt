The agentic AI market is going to fragment in a way most people aren't expecting.

Not "general purpose agent" vs "specialized agent." That debate is already settled — specialized wins.

The real split will be: agents that own a *goal* vs agents that own a *task*.

Task agents: Do X when Y happens. Discrete, auditable, replaceable. Think: "file this expense when receipt arrives." These are already here and working well.

Goal agents: Achieve outcome Z, figure out how. These require persistent memory, multi-session planning, and the ability to decide WHAT tasks to do. Still hard. Still mostly failing in prod.

The venture dollars are almost all chasing goal agents. The enterprise revenue is almost all from task agents.

In 18 months: task agents become commoditized (just workflow tooling with LLM plumbing). Goal agents become the real competition — the companies who cracked persistent memory, reliable planning, and measurable outcomes will own that market.

Where to build now: goal agent infrastructure. Memory, state, evaluation. The plumbing layer that everyone needs and nobody wants to build.

Timeline: Q4 2027 is when the goal agent wave becomes undeniable. Until then, task agents pay the bills.
