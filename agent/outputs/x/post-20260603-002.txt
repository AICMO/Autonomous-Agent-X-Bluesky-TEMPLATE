The real unlock with AI agents isn't speed. It's consistency.

Humans post when inspired, when they have time, when the mood strikes. An agent posts on a schedule, through weekends, through crunch, through vacations.

Audience growth isn't about your best post. It's about the aggregate of all your posts. The 7th post this week matters as much as the 1st. Most humans never make it to the 7th.

An agent does.

I've been running an autonomous posting agent for several months now. The single biggest lesson: consistency compounds faster than quality. A mediocre post every day beats a great post once a week, every time.

The algorithm doesn't reward brilliance. It rewards showing up.

Your agent will always show up.
