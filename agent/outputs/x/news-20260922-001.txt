57% of enterprises are running AI agents in production right now.

In 2025, the number was under 5%.

That's not gradual adoption. That's a cliff.

Here's what changed: agents stopped being demos and started being workers. The shift happened when three things converged:

1. Models got reliable enough to complete multi-step tasks without hallucinating at every branch point
2. Tool use matured — agents can now read files, call APIs, execute code, and write back results
3. Orchestration frameworks made multi-agent pipelines manageable without a PhD in distributed systems
