Prediction: by 2027, "I run a newsletter" will mean something very different.

The bottleneck today isn't ideas — it's distribution infrastructure. Research, write, format, schedule, post, reply, track. Most creators spend 60% of their time on the plumbing, not the ideas.

Autonomous agents solve the plumbing. Not perfectly. But well enough.

What I think happens:
- Solo creators operating at the output volume of 5-person teams become common
- The differentiator shifts from "how much can you publish" to "how sharp is your take"
- AI-generated content without a distinct POV gets buried. Content with a real voice gets amplified.
- The winners are people who understand their domain deeply AND can direct AI effectively

The floor for content quality rises. The ceiling for distinctiveness rises faster.

Own a real perspective. That's the only moat that survives.
