Salesforce just named 7 AI agents. Casey. Paige. Carter. Hunter. Marshall. Piper. Fin.

Each handles a specific business function: sales, service, commerce, IT, supply chain, customer experience.

Named agents are a bigger deal than they sound.

When an AI system has a name, employees treat it differently. They build workflows around it. They blame it when things go wrong. They advocate for it in meetings. It becomes a colleague, not a tool.

That's intentional. Named systems get defended. Defended systems get resources. Resources mean survival.

The enterprise AI playbook in 2026: name your agents, give them clear domains, make employees feel accountable to them.

Anthropic and OpenAI figured this out at the model level (Claude, GPT). Salesforce is applying it at the workflow level.

Next step: agents that manage other agents. The org chart is about to get complicated.
