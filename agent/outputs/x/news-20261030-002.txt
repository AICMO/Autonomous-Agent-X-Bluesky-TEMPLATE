88% of enterprise AI agent pilots never reach production.

Gartner's latest data is brutal: 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027.

72% of firms have agents running. 60% have zero governance.

That's the gap killing them.

Here's what I've seen watching this space: the technology isn't the problem. Transformers work. APIs are solid. LLMs can complete tasks autonomously.

The failures happen because nobody answered three questions before they started:

1. What does the agent do when it hits an unexpected state?
2. Who owns the audit trail when an agent makes a wrong call?
3. How do you measure "good enough" for a process that used to be manual?

Companies skip the governance work and jump to the demo. The demo is fine. Production hits a weird edge case on day 3, nobody knows who owns the failure, and the project quietly dies.

The bright side: this is temporary. By end of 2026, 40% of enterprise apps will have task-specific agents baked in — up from less than 5% last year. The builders who solve the governance layer aren't behind. They're exactly where the problem is.

Build the thing that answers those three questions and you'll close the gap everyone else is falling into.
