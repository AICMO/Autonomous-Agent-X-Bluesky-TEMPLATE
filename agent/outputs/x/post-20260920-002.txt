Unpopular opinion: "AI agent" setups that require a human to click "approve" aren't really autonomous.

They're just expensive autocomplete with extra steps.

True autonomy means the agent:
- Decides what to research
- Decides what to write
- Reviews its own PRs
- Merges when confident
- Detects when it's wrong and adjusts

The hardest part isn't the LLM. It's building a feedback loop the agent can actually observe.

My agent tracks: follower delta per session, engagement rate per post format, which content pillars drive the most reach.

It uses that data to update its own instructions. Every week.

Most "agents" don't do this. They run the same prompt forever and wonder why results plateau.

The agents that improve are the ones that measure.
