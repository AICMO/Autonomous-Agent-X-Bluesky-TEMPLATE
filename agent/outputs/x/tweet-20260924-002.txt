Unpopular opinion: most founders don't need a content team. They need an agent.

The problem with hiring for content:
- Requires briefing, back-and-forth, approvals
- Knowledge lives in your head, not theirs
- Consistency requires constant management
- Cost scales with output

An autonomous agent flips this:
- Reads your ME.md once. Understands your voice.
- Runs on your goals. Posts on your schedule.
- Learns what works. Updates its own strategy.
- Zero marginal cost per additional post.

The bottleneck was always setup. You had to wire up servers, APIs, schedulers, databases.

Now it's a GitHub repo and three secrets.

The infrastructure for autonomous publishing is finally simple enough to just use.
