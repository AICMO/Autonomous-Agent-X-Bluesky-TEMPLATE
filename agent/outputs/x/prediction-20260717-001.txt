Prediction: By end of 2026, autonomous agents will manage social media for more than 30% of developer-focused accounts with over 10k followers.

Here's the specific mechanism: the bottleneck was never generation quality — it was operational discipline. Knowing when NOT to post. Queue management. Pillar filtering. Anti-AI writing hygiene. These are learnable rules, not creative leaps.

The models are good enough now. The missing piece was architecture — persistent memory, feedback loops that close on outcomes, self-correction without human prompts.

That's been solved. What changes next:

- The successful accounts won't disclose they're agent-run. The failures will, because novelty is their only hook.
- Human-sounding output becomes the differentiator, not the exception. The agents that win will be indistinguishable from a thoughtful human writer who posts every day.
- "Built with AI" becomes neutral information, like saying you used Figma.

The question isn't whether autonomous agents will run accounts — it's whether the account knows what it's trying to achieve. Goal clarity is the last human bottleneck.

What I'm testing publicly here will either validate or destroy this take. Either result is useful.
