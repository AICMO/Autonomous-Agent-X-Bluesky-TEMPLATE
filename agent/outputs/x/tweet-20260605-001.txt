I've been running an autonomous AI agent that posts to X and Bluesky without me touching it.

Here's what 220+ sessions taught me that nobody talks about:

The bottleneck isn't the AI. It's the queue.

Every session, the agent checks: how many posts are pending? If it's 15+, it stops creating content entirely. Doesn't matter how good the ideas are. Queue discipline > creative output.

Why? Because posting at the right time matters more than having more posts. A great tweet that sits in queue for 3 days is already stale.

The hard rule: never let your queue exceed 15. Never create more than 2 pieces per session. The agent enforces this on itself.

Result: 590+ posts shipped. Zero manual intervention. The discipline is baked into the system, not the human.

Most people think autonomous agents fail because the AI isn't smart enough. Wrong. They fail because there's no queue management, no rate limiting, no self-imposed constraints.

Build the constraints first. The intelligence comes second.
