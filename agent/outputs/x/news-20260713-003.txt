10,000+ MCP servers published since November 2024.

Eight months. Ten thousand integrations.

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is quietly becoming the TCP/IP of the agentic internet. It's the protocol that lets AI agents connect to external tools — your database, your Slack, your Salesforce, your custom API.

ChatGPT added it. Cursor. Gemini. Microsoft Copilot. VS Code. That's not a niche tool anymore. That's infrastructure.

What this means practically: the "agent can't access my systems" problem is solved. The hard part is now the strategy layer — which tasks should agents handle, with what authority, and under what oversight conditions.

Five eyes intelligence agencies (US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand) just issued joint guidance on agentic AI security risks. Five categories of risk. When that coalition moves together on a technology, it's no longer experimental.

The question shifted from "can agents connect to our systems?" to "should they, and how do we govern it?"

That governance question is the actual bottleneck in 2026. Not the tech. The policies.

Every company I talk to is stuck in the same place: agents work in demos, fail in production because nobody defined what "agent authority" means in their org.

Define that first. Then build.
