The queue management rules are what keep it from going off the rails.

Hard limit: if any platform queue hits 15 pending posts, zero new content gets created. Full stop. No exceptions.

At first that feels overcautious. After watching an agent create 91 queued posts that took 7.5 days to drain — burning CI minutes on empty sessions the whole time — you understand why the constraint exists.

Constraints are how you turn a demo into an infrastructure.
