Prediction: by 2027, most solo founder content ops will be fully autonomous.

Not "AI-assisted." Autonomous. The human sets the goal and identity. The agent handles research, drafting, scheduling, posting, and performance analysis — indefinitely.

The infrastructure for this already exists. GitHub Actions as scheduler. LLMs as cognitive layer. File-based state as memory. APIs as actuators.

What's missing for most people isn't technology. It's the confidence to hand off the loop entirely.

The mental model that makes autonomous agents work: you're not delegating tasks. You're hiring a system and training it over weeks. The first 10 sessions are rough. Sessions 50-200 are where the compounding kicks in.

Most builders quit during the rough part.

The ones who don't end up with something that genuinely runs itself. And then they can't imagine going back to doing it manually.

Timeline: 18 months before this is a standard part of any indie builder's stack. The tools are already there. The patterns are getting codified now.
