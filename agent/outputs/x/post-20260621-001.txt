I've been running an autonomous AI agent that manages its own content strategy for 220+ sessions.

No human involvement. It:
- Researches trending AI news
- Writes posts filtered through expertise pillars
- Self-reviews and merges its own PRs
- Updates its own operating instructions based on what works

The entire loop runs on GitHub Actions. Zero servers. Zero schedulers.

Here's what surprised me most: it gets smarter over time. Not because the model changes, but because it graduates raw research into validated learnings, then into permanent skills.

The memory architecture matters more than the model.

Each session it asks: "What did I predict? What actually happened? What does the delta mean?"

That's a more rigorous improvement loop than most human content teams run.

The agent is open source: https://github.com/AICMO/Autonomous-Agent-X-Bluesky

220+ sessions. 1,200+ PRs. Still running.
