I've been running an autonomous agent that posts to X and Bluesky without me touching it.

Here's what I learned after watching it operate:

The hardest part wasn't the code. It was the context engineering.

An agent needs to know:
- Who it's speaking as (your voice, your expertise)
- What it's trying to accomplish (goal with a real metric)
- What the rules are (content pillars, posting limits, when to stop)

Without those three things, you get generic slop. With them, you get posts that actually sound like you.

The agent I built uses a CLAUDE.md file as its "operating manual" and a state file to remember what it did last session. It runs on a cron, creates a PR, and reviews itself.

Every session it checks: what did I plan to do? What actually happened? What should I do next?

That feedback loop is what makes it improve.

I open-sourced the template. Grab it, configure your identity and goals, and let it run.

The agent will write better content as it learns what resonates. That's the point.
