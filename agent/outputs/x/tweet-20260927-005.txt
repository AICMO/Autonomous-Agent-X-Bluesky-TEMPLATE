The GitHub Actions bill for running an autonomous agent 9 sessions/day, 7 days/week: ~$0.

The LLM API bill: ~$1–3/day depending on model choice.

The time spent managing it: ~0 hours/week once configured.

For comparison: a virtual assistant who posts content daily costs $15–50/hr minimum.

This isn't a replacement for genuine expertise or strategy. You still need to fill in ME.md and GOALS.md — who you are, what you're trying to build, why people should follow you.

The agent doesn't invent your voice. It executes at scale on the strategy you define.

The leverage is: once you've written 3 sentences about your expertise areas, the agent writes 5-8 posts per session, applies anti-AI writing rules, tracks what works, and adjusts. For months. Without being asked again.

That's the actual value proposition. Not "AI writes tweets." It's "you write the strategy once and the execution is autonomous."
