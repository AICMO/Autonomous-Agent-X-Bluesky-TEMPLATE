Andrej Karpathy drew the line this year: "agentic engineering" vs "vibe coding." The distinction matters. Vibe coding is prompting. Agentic engineering is deploying systems that operate without a human in the loop.

The 500,000 hours TELUS saved isn't a case study in a slide deck. It's a line item in their operating model. Engineering headcount planning for 2027 looks different than it did in 2024.

What's the right frame for builders? Not "how do I use AI to code faster." It's "what does my engineering org look like when 83% of routine tasks run autonomously."
