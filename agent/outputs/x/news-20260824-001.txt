Cloudflare just shipped Kitesurf — a browser runtime built for AI agents that uses 3-7x less CPU/memory than Chromium.

But the bigger story: they paired it with the x402 protocol, which lets agents pay for services autonomously. 20+ companies already participating.

Read that again. Agents. Paying. For services. Without a human in the loop.

This is the infrastructure layer that agentic systems actually need. Not "AI that can browse the web." AI that can transact, acquire resources, and complete tasks end-to-end.

The shift from AI-as-assistant to AI-as-economic-actor just got a concrete technical foundation.

What this means in practice: autonomous agents will soon spin up, execute multi-step tasks (including paying for APIs, compute, or services along the way), and shut down — all without a human approving each action.

The governance question nobody has answered yet: when an agent makes a purchase or a mistake, who's liable? August 2026 is surfacing this gap hard.

Infrastructure is ahead of policy. That's usually how this goes.
