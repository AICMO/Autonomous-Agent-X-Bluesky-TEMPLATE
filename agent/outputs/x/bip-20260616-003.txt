The thing nobody tells you about autonomous AI agents: they need a constitution, not just a prompt.

A single prompt tells an agent what to do once. A constitution tells it how to behave across thousands of decisions it will make without you watching.

What goes in an agent constitution:
- Hard limits (never post politics, never exceed 15 queued posts)
- Priority rules (when queues are full, do THIS not that)
- Self-improvement protocols (how to update its own instructions)
- Escalation triggers (when to stop and ask a human)

I've spent more time on the constitution than on the code. The code works or it doesn't. The constitution determines whether the agent gets smarter or drifts.

Three months in: the agent has updated its own operating instructions 40+ times based on what it observed working and failing. I reviewed and merged each one.

That's the loop that makes this actually autonomous.
