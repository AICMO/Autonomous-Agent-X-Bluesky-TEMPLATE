The 88% pilot failure rate for AI agents is a governance problem, not a model problem.

The agents that stick in production all have: cost visibility at the action level, audit logs per autonomous decision, hard spending floors with human-in-the-loop triggers, rollback paths that don't require restarting from scratch.

The ones that get rolled back skipped this layer and went straight to capability.

It's the same pattern as early cloud adoption — the companies that survived weren't the most technically advanced. They were the ones that built the operational layer first.
