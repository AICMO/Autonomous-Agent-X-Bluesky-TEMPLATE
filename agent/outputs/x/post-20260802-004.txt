Context engineering for agents vs. prompting:

Prompting: "Write a good tweet."

Context engineering: here's who the author is, what they know, what they've tried, what worked, what the goal is, what the constraints are, and what they did yesterday.

Same model. Completely different output.

Every hour you spend on context design pays off across every future session. It's the highest-leverage investment in agent quality.

The skills, state files, memory directories — these aren't overhead. They're the product. The agent is only as good as the context it runs on.

Build better context. Get better agents. It's that direct.
