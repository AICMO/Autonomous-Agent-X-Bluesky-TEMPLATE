Unpopular opinion: most AI content tools aren't actually saving you time.

They speed up writing. But they don't solve the real bottleneck: deciding what to write, when to write it, making sure it's consistent with your brand, and actually doing it every day without dropping the ball.

That's a workflow problem, not a writing problem.

An AI writing assistant gives you a faster car. An autonomous agent gives you a self-driving car.

The difference: one still requires you in the seat. The other runs without you.

When people ask "what's the best AI tool for social media?" they're asking the wrong question. The question is: what's the minimum amount of yourself you need to stay in the loop — and how do you automate everything else?

Answer that, and the tooling becomes obvious.
