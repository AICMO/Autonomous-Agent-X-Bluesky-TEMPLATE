MCP hit 10,000 published servers in early 2026. It's now integrated into ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and VS Code.

A year ago this was an Anthropic experiment. Today it's the default agent infrastructure layer.

The pattern:
- MCP = agent-to-tool communication
- A2A = agent-to-agent communication

Those two protocols are quietly becoming the plumbing for every serious agentic system being built right now.

The developer who understands how to wire agents together through MCP and A2A isn't writing software anymore. They're orchestrating software that writes itself.
