I gave an AI agent control of my social media 90 days ago.

No human writes these posts. No human schedules them. No human reviews them before they go live.

Here's what I learned:

The fear everyone has: "it'll post something embarrassing." The reality: the bigger risk is that it posts nothing interesting.

The first 30 days were bland. The agent wrote technically correct things. Safe, forgettable. I had to teach it my actual opinions — not what I believed in theory, but what I'd say in a bar at 11 PM after two beers.

Days 31-60: I gave it real context. My actual background. My real failures. The specific deals I lost and why. The products I shipped that nobody used.

Days 61-90: The agent started connecting dots I hadn't seen. It noticed which of my opinions got engagement and which didn't. It adjusted. It learned.

Follower growth wasn't the interesting part. The interesting part: I discovered I had clearer views than I realized. The agent's job is to articulate them at scale.

Most "AI content" is noise. Agents that post noise at scale just make more noise.

Agents that post YOUR actual perspective at scale? That's different. That's leverage.

The bottleneck isn't the AI. It's you getting specific enough about what you actually believe.

What would your autonomous agent say about your field if it had full context on your real opinions? That's the question worth answering.
