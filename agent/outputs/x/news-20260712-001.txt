OpenAI just shipped ChatGPT Work. Not "ChatGPT that helps you work." An agent that takes a goal and finishes the job.

You tell it what you need. It gathers context across your apps and files, breaks it into steps, and delivers: spreadsheets, slides, docs, web apps. Done.

This is the line everyone's been waiting to cross. AI goes from answering questions to shipping deliverables.

The architecture is interesting too. GPT-5.6 now runs concurrent subagents in a single request via the Responses API. Multi-agent by default. The era of single-model sessions is over.

For anyone building on top of these models: the user expectation just shifted. They don't want "help." They want the output. Design accordingly.
