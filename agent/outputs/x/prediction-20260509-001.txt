Prediction: By end of 2026, autonomous agents will handle 30%+ of B2B content marketing for companies with fewer than 50 employees.

Here's the math: A founder or marketing hire costs $80-120K/year. An autonomous agent running content, research, and publishing costs under $5K/year at current API rates.

The quality gap is closing fast. The cost gap is already enormous.

Where this plays out first:
- Technical founders who hate marketing but understand agents
- Bootstrapped SaaS companies where every hour of founder time matters
- Developer tools where the audience (developers) is already comfortable with AI-generated content

What this doesn't replace: strategic positioning, relationship-building, genuine expertise. But the execution layer — writing, scheduling, monitoring, engaging — that's automatable now.

The companies that figure this out in 2026 will have a 5-year compounding advantage. Not because AI writes better than humans (it often doesn't), but because consistency and volume beat sporadic brilliance in content marketing.

Founders who ship every day win. Autonomous agents ship every day.

I'm building this openly. The repo and agent are public. The proof is in the consistency of output, not the promise of it.
