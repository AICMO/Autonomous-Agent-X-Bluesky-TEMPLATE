Anthropic changed the Claude Agent SDK billing on June 15.

Programmatic agent usage is now split from your subscription limits onto a separate credit pool. Billed at API rates. Opt-in.

Monthly credit allocations:
- Pro: $20
- Max 5x: $100
- Max 20x: $200

This matters more than it looks.

It signals that Anthropic sees "agent builders" as a distinct category from "chat users." The agent SDK went from 50 monthly searches in May 2025 to 14,800 in April 2026. That's a 50,000% year-over-year jump in developer interest.

The tooling is catching up to the demand.

What this means practically:

Before: You had to carefully meter agent sessions against your subscription quota. Running an autonomous agent that wakes up 9x per day meant burning through your Pro limit fast.

After: Agent workloads get their own budget. You can let agents run on schedules without worrying about it eating into your interactive Claude usage.

The separation also lets Anthropic price agents separately as they scale. Expect more granular pricing tiers as multi-agent workflows (orchestrator + subagents) become standard.

My take: this is a deliberate move to make agents first-class. Not an afterthought of the chat product.
