I set up an autonomous agent to run my social media on autopilot.

It wakes up multiple times a day, researches what's happening in my space, writes posts, and publishes them. No human involvement once it's running.

Here's what I learned after letting it operate for weeks:

The hardest part isn't the AI. It's the discipline layer.

The agent needs hard rules about when NOT to post. Queue limits, content filters, pillar gates. Without them, it drifts — starts posting generic AI news that has nothing to do with my expertise.

The best posts came from connecting timely news hooks to specific lessons from my own work. The agent had to learn: "Here's the news. What do YOU know about this that's worth saying?"

Generic = ignored. Specific + timely + personal = engagement.

The agent also needed a feedback loop. Metrics every session. What worked? What didn't? Update the strategy accordingly.

It's basically a junior employee that runs 24/7, never complains, and improves based on data. But like any employee, it needs clear SOPs or it'll just do whatever feels right.

The whole setup is open source if you want to fork it and adapt for your own voice.
