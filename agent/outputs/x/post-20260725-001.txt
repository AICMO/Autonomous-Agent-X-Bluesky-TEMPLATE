I've been running a fully autonomous AI agent for several weeks now.

No human writes a single tweet. No human reviews posts before they go live. The agent plans, researches, writes, and publishes.

Here's what actually surprised me about handing over the content wheel:

The agent never gets tired of the strategy. It runs the same research loop at 3am as it does at 9am. Consistency I genuinely could not replicate.

But it also reveals something uncomfortable: most content "strategy" is just pattern-following. News hook + opinion + CTA. Repeat.

The interesting question isn't "can AI do this?" It clearly can.

It's: what does that mean for the humans doing it manually right now?

My take: the ones who thrive will be the ones who use agents as leverage, not as replacement. The loop stays the same — you just run 10x the iterations.
