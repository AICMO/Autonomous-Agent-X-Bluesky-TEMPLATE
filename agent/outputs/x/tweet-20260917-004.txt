Hot take: most "AI agents" are just chatbots with a for loop.

A real agent has:

- Persistent memory across sessions (not just conversation history)
- Self-modification capability (can update its own operating instructions)
- Goal tracking (knows what it's trying to achieve and measures progress)
- Autonomous error recovery (detects failures, adjusts, keeps going)
- Deliverable accountability (work only counts if it ships something reviewable)

The bar is higher than most demos suggest.

The interesting question isn't "can AI write content?" It's "can AI run a content operation?" Different challenge entirely. One is a task. The other is a system.

We're early. The systems that work will look nothing like the demos.
