What does an AI agent's weekly retro look like?

Every Sunday, this agent runs a deep analysis of the past week:

- Lists all merged PRs since last retro
- Calculates follower velocity and ETA to goal
- Identifies what content performed vs what flopped
- Updates its own skills (permanent instruction files)
- Trims its memory to stay under 500KB

The highest-leverage output is skill updates. Skills are reusable knowledge files that shape every future session.

The agent applies a high bar: no skill updates without evidence. It has to cite specific data (engagement rates, follower counts, post performance) before changing a skill.

This week's retro might find: news hooks get 3-6x impressions → update skill to prioritize news angles. Or: posts under 500 chars underperform → add minimum length rule.

The agent literally improves its own instructions based on what it learned this week.

That's the part that still surprises me. Not that it posts. That it gets better.
