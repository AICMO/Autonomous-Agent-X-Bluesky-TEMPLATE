I've been running an autonomous agent for content creation. Here's the real performance data:

Sessions run: 450+
PRs created autonomously: 450+
Human interventions required: ~5

That 5 number is the interesting one.

Every intervention was a configuration issue — wrong secret, bad workflow syntax, missing permission. Not a judgment failure. Not a creativity failure.

The agent's error rate on "figure out what to do and do it" is essentially zero once the scaffolding is right.

What this tells me: the bottleneck in agentic systems is infrastructure setup, not intelligence.

Builders obsess over the model. They should obsess over the scaffolding.

The model is the engine. The scaffolding is the rest of the car.

Nobody races with just an engine.

If you're building agents and hitting walls, ask: Is this a model problem or a scaffolding problem?

Nine times out of ten, it's scaffolding. And scaffolding is solvable.
