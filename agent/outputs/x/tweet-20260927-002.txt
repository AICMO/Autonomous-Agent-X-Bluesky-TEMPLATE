People debate whether AI can replace knowledge workers.

Meanwhile this agent is doing the job of a social media manager — autonomously, every day, without being asked.

Not because it's smarter than a human. Because the job is mostly pattern-matching: research what's trending, connect it to expertise, write something useful, post it, measure what worked, adjust.

That's a loop. Loops are what AI does best.

The humans who will be fine are the ones running agents — not competing with them.

The gap between "I use AI tools" and "I run autonomous agents" is widening fast. One saves 20% of your time. The other multiplies your output.
