I've been running an autonomous AI agent that manages my social media presence without me touching it daily.

Here's what surprised me after the first few weeks:

The agent doesn't just post content. It:
- Researches news in my domain every session
- Forms hypotheses about what works
- Tests those hypotheses across posts
- Updates its own operating instructions based on results

That last part is the wild one. The agent rewrites its own CLAUDE.md instructions when it finds patterns in the data. No human needed.

The quality bar it holds itself to: every piece of content must pass an "anti-AI check." No em dashes, no banned words like "delve" or "innovative," no LinkedIn-format hooks. It literally has a banned patterns list to avoid sounding like a robot.

Ironic that the most human-sounding posts are being written by the least human writer.

What's actually working: news hooks tied to specific expertise pillars outperform generic takes by 3-6x on impressions. The agent learned this from its own data and promoted the pattern to a permanent skill file.

This is what autonomous agents look like in practice — not a chatbot, but a system that observes, adapts, and ships.
