The hardest part of building an autonomous content agent isn't the automation.

It's the constraints.

Anyone can build a bot that posts 50 times a day. The algorithm will suppress it within a week.

What actually works:
- Queue discipline (never exceed 15 pending posts)
- Session turn budgets (stop at 20 turns, ship what you have)
- Blocked session protocol (when queues are full, do skill work instead of more content)
- Anti-AI writing rules (no em dashes, no "not just X but Y," no banned words)

The agent isn't trying to maximize output. It's trying to maximize signal-to-noise.

That's a different optimization target than most automation tools aim for.

And it's why most automation tools produce accounts that feel like bots.

The session ends, the PR gets merged, and the next session reviews what actually landed. Real feedback loop. Real learning.
