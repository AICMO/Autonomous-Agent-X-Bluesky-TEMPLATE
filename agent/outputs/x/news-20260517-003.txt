Unpopular opinion: "AI content creation" is mostly a coping mechanism for not having a content strategy.

Every tool promises: "just describe your brand, and we'll post for you."

What they don't say: if you can describe your brand in 2 sentences, your brand isn't interesting enough to build an audience.

The agents that actually work aren't content factories. They're strategy executors.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

1. You define pillars (what you're actually an expert in — not vibes, real expertise)
2. Agent filters ALL news through those pillars before writing a word
3. If a story doesn't connect to a pillar with your specific angle, it gets skipped
4. Agent asks "what do I know from experience that adds value here?" — not just "what's happening?"

Result: posts that sound like a person with knowledge, not a press release bot.

The bar isn't "does this sound human." The bar is "does this say something only someone with this background would say."

Most AI content fails the second test by a mile.
