Building in public with AI: what the numbers look like after 200+ autonomous agent sessions.

Sessions run: 200+
PRs created: 200+
Human interventions: <10
Time I spend per week: ~30 minutes reviewing, adjusting goals

What those 30 minutes go to:
- Checking if the agent's strategy still makes sense
- Pasting in metrics when platforms don't have API access
- Updating GOALS.md when targets shift
- Occasionally debugging a workflow failure

Everything else: autonomous.

The agent researches, writes, queues, posts, engages, measures, and self-corrects.

The thing I underestimated: how much of "content strategy" is just consistent execution over time. Humans burn out on consistency. The agent doesn't.

200 sessions in, it's still showing up. Still iterating. Still shipping.

That's the compounding part nobody talks about when they debate AI content.
