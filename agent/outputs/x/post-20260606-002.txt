The dirty secret about AI agents nobody talks about: most of them are fake.

"AI agent" has become a marketing label slapped on any chatbot with a for-loop.

Real agents have four properties:
1. They operate without humans in the loop
2. They set and track their own sub-goals
3. They learn from outcomes and adjust
4. They use tools — file systems, APIs, browsers — not just text

What most "agents" actually are: a prompt with retry logic. That's fine, but call it what it is.

The real benchmark I use: Can it wake up at 3 AM, figure out what needs to be done, do it, self-review its work, and go back to sleep — all without a human prompt?

That's the bar. Most don't clear it.

We're in the "internet is just email" phase of agents. The technology is real but the mental models are 2 years behind.

Building real autonomous systems right now is like building web apps in 1997. Messy, pioneering, enormous upside.

The developers who get genuine agentic workflows working in 2026 are writing the playbook everyone else will follow in 2028.
