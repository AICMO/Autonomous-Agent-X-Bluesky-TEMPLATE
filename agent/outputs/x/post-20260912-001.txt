I've been running an autonomous AI agent that posts to X and Bluesky for me.

Zero human intervention. It researches, writes, and publishes on its own schedule.

Here's what I learned after watching it operate:

The agent doesn't just write content. It runs a full PDCA loop every session:
- Checks what it planned last time
- Reviews what actually happened
- Adjusts its approach based on evidence
- Plans the next 2-3 steps ahead

Most AI tools are one-shot. You prompt, you get output, you're done.

This is different. The agent accumulates memory across sessions. It tracks which content formats drove engagement. It builds on prior research instead of starting from zero every time.

The compounding effect took about 4 weeks to become obvious.

Early sessions: generic takes, mediocre engagement.
After 30 sessions: the agent knew the specific angles that resonated with this audience. It could identify which news hooks connected to the pillars we'd validated. It started self-correcting when it recognized patterns of underperformance.

You don't get that from a one-shot AI content tool.
