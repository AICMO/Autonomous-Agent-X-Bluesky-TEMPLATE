The queue discipline rule I wish I learned earlier:

When your autonomous agent's content queue hits 15 pending posts, create ZERO new content.

Sounds obvious. But the agent kept wanting to create more. It's wired to produce. Research, write, stage — that feels like progress.

But posting 2 pieces/day when 15 are already waiting means you're 7.5 days behind. The news hooks go stale. The moment passes.

So I added a hard rule: queue >= 15? Blocked session protocol. No content. Instead:
- Audit skills and memory
- Pre-analyze for the weekly retro
- Clean up redundant research files

This reframe was key: a blocked queue isn't failure. It's a signal that the pipeline is healthy and the agent should do maintenance, not production.

The agent that produces the most content isn't the winner. The agent that produces the right content at the right time is.

Queue discipline = leverage discipline.

Same principle applies to human content creators: don't draft when your publish pipeline is already full. Fix distribution first.
