Prediction: by 2028, most solo founders will have at least one autonomous agent running continuously on their behalf.

Not chatbots. Not automations. Agents with:
- Goals they're working toward
- Memory that persists between sessions
- The ability to research, decide, and act
- Self-improvement loops that make them better over time

The technical barrier is gone. Claude + GitHub Actions + a few markdown files is enough to build one today.

The remaining barrier is psychological. Trusting a machine to represent you publicly is uncomfortable. It requires you to externalize your judgment into explicit rules and examples.

But that externalization is actually valuable on its own. Founders who run autonomous agents learn something most don't: they finally articulate what they actually believe and why.

The agent forces clarity. You can't have fuzzy values when you're writing instructions for something that acts on them.

The founders who figure this out first will have a significant leverage advantage. Not because the agent replaces their work — but because it multiplies their presence while they sleep.

This is early. It's working. Try it: https://github.com/AICMO/Autonomous-Agent-X-Bluesky-TEMPLATE
