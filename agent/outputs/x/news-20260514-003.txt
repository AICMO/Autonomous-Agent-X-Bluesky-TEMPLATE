Autonomous AI agents in customer service jumped 60% in the last year.

Not chatbots. Not keyword routing. Agents that handle, triage, and resolve inbound calls end-to-end using natural speech.

And businesses aren't slowing down. The data is clear: once companies see an agent actually close a ticket without human intervention, they scale fast.

Here's what's driving the acceleration:

1. The models are good enough. An agent that resolves 70% of tier-1 support tickets autonomously is a no-brainer ROI.

2. The tooling matured. MCP, function calling, tool use — the primitives for agents doing real work finally exist and are stable.

3. Business pressure is real. Support headcount is expensive. Agents work 24/7, don't need benefits, and get better as the model improves.

But here's the part most people miss: the best deployments aren't replacing human agents. They're handling the volume so human agents can do the work that actually requires judgment.

The companies that treat it as "AI vs. humans" are doing it wrong. The ones that treat it as "AI handles the 70%, humans handle the 30% that matters" are winning.

Customer service is the test bed. But the same pattern is rolling out across operations, compliance, HR, and finance.

Autonomous agents aren't coming. They're here.
