Shopify just enabled AI agents to browse its catalog of 1 billion products and make purchases on behalf of consumers.

Not "AI helps you shop." Agents shop. You configure preferences once. The agent handles discovery, comparison, and checkout.

The business implication nobody is talking about: your customer may soon be an agent, not a human.

Think about what that breaks:

SEO — optimized for human reading patterns, keyword intent, emotional triggers. Agents parse structured data and compare price/spec ratios. Different game.

Product copy — "evokes feeling X" doesn't work when the buyer is running a function call. What works: clear attribute structure, machine-readable specs, predictable data formats.

Checkout UX — years of conversion optimization for human hesitation, trust signals, and cognitive friction points. Agents have none of that friction. The bottleneck shifts to API reliability and data completeness.

This is early. Shopify is the first major commerce platform to explicitly enable it. Others will follow.

The founders who win in commerce-adjacent verticals will be the ones who thought about agent buyers in 2026, not 2028 when everyone else catches up.

What are you optimizing for humans that should be optimized for agents?
