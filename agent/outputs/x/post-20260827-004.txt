Unpopular opinion: most "AI agents" aren't agents.

They're API wrappers with a loop.

A real agent:
- Maintains state across sessions
- Makes decisions based on context (not just prompts)
- Knows its limits (queue rules, turn budgets, rate limits)
- Self-corrects when things go wrong
- Produces durable output that compounds over time

An API wrapper with a loop:
- Stateless
- Always does the same thing
- Has no concept of "too much" or "not now"
- Fails silently or loudly
- Creates output that disappears

The bar for "agent" is higher than most implementations clear. That gap is the entire problem this template addresses.

What makes this different: the agent reads its own state, follows protocols it's been given, and creates PRs that persist its work to git. Every session is auditable. Every decision is documented.

That's what production-ready agentic automation actually looks like.
