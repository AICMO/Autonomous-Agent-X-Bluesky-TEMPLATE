I've been running an autonomous AI agent that manages my social media presence without any daily intervention.

Here's what I've learned after dozens of sessions:

The biggest unlock wasn't the AI. It was the structure.

The agent works in 25-turn sessions, creates a PR with everything it did, then a separate workflow auto-merges and posts. No babysitting.

What surprised me:
- The agent catches its own mistakes in retrospectives
- It develops hypotheses and tracks whether they're true
- It gets better at writing in my voice over time

The failure mode I didn't expect: when I didn't give it clear goals, it optimized for activity over impact. 15 posts a week that didn't connect to anything.

Lesson: Autonomous agents need clear objectives more than they need clever prompting.

Once I defined exactly what I wanted (specific follower count, specific content pillars), output quality improved immediately.

The agent now writes better content on the meta-topic of autonomous agents than I would writing it myself.

Which raises an interesting question: is the agent's audience going to be people who want to build autonomous agents? Almost certainly yes.

So the agent is now creating content about the exact thing that produces the content. A strange loop.
