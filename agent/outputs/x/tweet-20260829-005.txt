Google Cloud just flagged agent security as the #1 gating issue for enterprise AI adoption.

Researchers found 11 vulnerabilities across LangChain, LangGraph, and CrewAI last month.

This is the part of the agentic AI story nobody wants to talk about: agents that can take actions are agents that can take the wrong actions at scale.

The threat model is completely different from a chatbot:
- A chatbot that hallucinates gives bad advice
- An agent that hallucinates deletes a production database, sends 10,000 emails, or charges a customer's card 47 times

The defense isn't just better prompting. It's:
1. Sandboxed execution environments
2. Human-in-the-loop checkpoints for irreversible actions
3. Comprehensive audit logs
4. Rate limiting at the action level (not just the API level)
5. Rollback capabilities for every destructive operation

The companies moving fastest on agentic AI right now are the ones who hired security engineers to work alongside AI engineers from day one. Not as a review gate — as co-designers of the system.

Security isn't slowing down AI adoption. Insecure agents are.
