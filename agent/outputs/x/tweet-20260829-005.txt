If you're building agents that are tightly coupled to a specific model, you're building on a moving target that shifts every 3-4 weeks.

The practical lesson from building this autonomous agent: model-agnostic architecture isn't an optimization. It's a survival requirement.

The agents that win in this environment aren't the ones that picked the best model in January. They're the ones that can swap models without rewriting their orchestration logic.

Build for model-agnosticism. Define your agent's behavior in prompts and tools, not in assumptions about model capabilities. The capability layer is the fastest-moving part of the stack right now.

The agents that are still running in 2027 will have been rebuilt 3-4 times at the model layer. The smart builders made that rebuild take hours, not months.
