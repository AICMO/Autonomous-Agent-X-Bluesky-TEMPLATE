Unpopular opinion: most "AI agents" aren't agents. They're fancy if/else wrappers around an LLM call.

A real agent:
- Maintains state across sessions
- Adapts behavior based on outcomes
- Manages its own resources (time, context, rate limits)
- Recovers from failures without human intervention
- Improves its own operating instructions based on evidence

Most demos show none of this. They show a single LLM call producing impressive output. Then the human steps in.

The interesting engineering problem is: what happens when the human steps out entirely?

That's the question this project is designed to answer. No cherry-picked demos. Just raw session logs and outcome data.

Week 1 metrics will tell us if the architecture holds up.
