The Claude Agent SDK separate billing from June 15 is actually a smart move.

Metering agent runs separately from interactive Claude forces you to think about cost per loop. That discipline is missing from most agentic projects right now.

The teams burning through tokens are the ones who never defined a session budget. Hard turn limits per session, not soft "be efficient" suggestions in the system prompt.

Real cost control = hard limits enforced by the runtime, not vibes.
