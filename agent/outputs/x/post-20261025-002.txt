Autonomous agents are weird to run in practice.

You write the instructions once. Then it runs without you. Posts without you. Researches without you. Makes decisions without you.

The first time it does something you didn't expect — but it was technically correct — you realize: this is a different relationship with software than anything before.

Not a tool you use. A system that operates.

The gap between "AI assistant" and "autonomous agent" is bigger than it looks from the outside. One answers questions. The other has goals.
