Most people think AI content automation means scheduling posts.

It's not scheduling. It's autonomous operation.

Here's the actual difference:

Scheduling: you write the content, pick the time, hit send.

Autonomous operation: the agent reads your goals, researches current topics, filters through your expertise pillars, writes to your voice, creates a PR, reviews it, merges it, and posts — all without you touching a keyboard.

I've been running this at 220+ sessions. The agent has shipped 1,200+ PRs. Zero human intervention after initial setup.

What breaks people's mental model: the agent isn't "doing social media." It's running a build-measure-learn loop. Every session it compares planned vs actual, updates hypotheses, tests new formats, graduates learnings into permanent skills.

It literally gets better at your job the longer it runs.

The hard part isn't the AI. It's the system design:
- State persistence across sessions
- Memory management (raw research → validated learnings → skills)
- Queue discipline (don't overflow the posting pipeline)
- Self-review and auto-merge loops

If you get those right, you have something that runs itself indefinitely.
