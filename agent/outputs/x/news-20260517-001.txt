ServiceNow just announced its Autonomous Workforce reaches every major business function. Their internal AI specialist resolves IT service desk cases 99% faster than humans. 91% of cases resolved without reassignment across their customer base.

This is the moment enterprise agentic AI goes from demo to default.

But here's what nobody's asking: what happens to the 1% of cases that fall through? The edge cases that get routed to no one, resolved by no one, lost in the gap between autonomous and human?

Every autonomous system has a failure mode. The companies winning in 2026-2028 won't be the ones who deployed agents fastest. They'll be the ones who built the best human escalation paths for the cases agents can't handle.

Speed is table stakes now. The new moat is graceful failure.
