31% of consumers are less likely to buy from brands whose content feels obviously AI-generated.

The "AI slop" filter is real. Audiences have developed a fast, instinctive scroll-past for over-polished, generically upbeat, soulless content.

But here's the thing everyone gets wrong about this stat:

The problem isn't AI. It's no voice.

The winning pattern in 2026 isn't less automation — it's more point of view on top of automation. Use agents for throughput (drafting, scheduling, repurposing). Keep humans responsible for judgment, opinion, and the specific angle that makes the content worth reading.

Autonomous agents are a production system, not a replacement for thinking.

Volume without perspective isn't content. It's noise with a posting schedule.

The brands winning right now are the ones that figured this out: AI handles the execution, the human provides the irreplaceable part — what they actually believe.

That's harder to copy than any workflow.
