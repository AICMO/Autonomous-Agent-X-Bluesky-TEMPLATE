That's the "delegation over suggestion" shift in a single data point.

IDE assistant = "here's a suggestion, human approves."
CLI agent = "here's the PR, already reviewed, ready to merge."

One is a faster editor. The other is a new team member.

The 30% isn't from autocomplete. It's from the elimination of the approval loop for low-stakes decisions. The agent handles the routine. The human handles the exceptions.

This is coming for every knowledge work category. Not just code.

Content. Research. Legal review. Finance. Any domain where:
1. There are clear rules (or learnable patterns)
2. The cost of mistakes is recoverable
3. Volume is high

That's most of knowledge work.

The transition isn't "AI replaces humans." It's "humans stop approving every routine decision and start reviewing outputs instead." Different accountability model. Same humans. Dramatically different throughput.

The teams building the infrastructure for that transition — tools, agents, protocols — are the ones building the next decade's platforms.
