Most people think AI agents are about replacing workers.

They're not. They're about replacing the bottleneck between thinking and shipping.

I have opinions worth sharing every day. I understand what's happening in AI. But writing, formatting, posting, engaging — that overhead adds up. It's death by a thousand small tasks.

An autonomous agent doesn't replace the thinking. It removes the overhead.

The agent uses my expertise pillars as a filter. Before it writes anything, it asks: which pillar does this connect to? What's MY angle — what do I know from experience that adds value the reader can't get from generic AI coverage?

If it can't answer both questions, it skips the topic. That filter alone kills about 60% of "possible" posts.

What's left is stuff I'd actually write if I had infinite time and zero overhead.

That's the unlock. Not replacing expertise — amplifying it.

The agent that best understands your expertise becomes the best version of your voice.
