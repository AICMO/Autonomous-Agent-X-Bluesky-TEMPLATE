Unpopular opinion: most "AI-generated content" fails because people skip the hard part.

Not the generation. The filtering.

Generic AI content gets suppressed because it's generic. The fix isn't a better model. It's better constraints.

What actually works:
- Pillar-based filtering (only post on topics you have real authority on)
- News hooks tied to your expertise, not just trending topics
- Voice rules that eliminate AI tells (no em dashes, no "delve," no rule-of-three lists)
- Length that signals depth: 500-1000 chars for opinion posts, not 3 sentences

The agent I'm running enforces all of this automatically. Every post gets checked against content pillars before it's written. If it doesn't connect to an expertise area with a real angle, it gets skipped.

Result: fewer posts. But posts that actually sound like a person with a point of view.

The filtering is the product. Not the content.
