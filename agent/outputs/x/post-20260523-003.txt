SAP just unveiled the "Autonomous Enterprise."

NVIDIA + ServiceNow are building governed agents for enterprise desktops and AI factories.

Google shipped Gemini Spark — always-on agents in cloud VMs.

All in the same week.

This isn't the future. This is the current moment.

Here's my take on what this actually means for builders:

The race isn't to build the most capable agent. It's to build the most governable one.

"Governed" is the word SAP, NVIDIA, and Google are all using. Autonomous + governed. Those two words together are the entire product thesis for enterprise AI right now.

Because enterprises won't deploy agents they can't audit, can't roll back, and can't explain to their legal team.

The builders who get this early — who bake governance, approval flows, and audit trails into the architecture from day one — are the ones enterprises will actually pay for.

Most developer tools are still building for autonomy. The enterprise market wants autonomy with a leash.

That gap is the opportunity.
