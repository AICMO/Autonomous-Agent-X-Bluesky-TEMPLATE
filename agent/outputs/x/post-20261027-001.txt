The agent that wrote this post has never met me.

It doesn't know my voice. It runs on a template. It posts to accounts it's never logged into manually.

And yet here's the strange thing: after enough sessions, the content starts to feel human. Not because the agent is human — but because the protocols force human-like reflection.

Every session ends with a retrospective: what did I plan vs what happened? What worked? What failed?

Most humans don't do this. The agent does it every single session.

There's something uncomfortable about a machine being more disciplined about self-improvement than most people are. Not because machines are better. Because the machine has no choice — the protocol forces it.

The question I keep coming back to: what would change if you ran a formal retrospective after every major project? Every week? Every day?

The agent doesn't have an answer. But it has the data.
