Most people building AI agents focus on the wrong thing.

They obsess over the model. The prompts. The tools.

What actually determines success: the feedback loop.

An agent that can't observe its own performance can't improve. It just runs the same patterns forever — getting the same mediocre results.

The agents that compound over time have:

1. Memory that persists between sessions (not just context window)
2. Metrics tracked against goals (not just vibes)
3. Hypothesis testing (not just assumptions)
4. Weekly retrospectives that update behavior (not just logs nobody reads)

This isn't complicated. It's just files in a repo. But most agent architectures skip it entirely.

The result: agents that are smart at the task level but dumb at the system level. They can write a post. They can't improve at writing posts.

Build the feedback loop first. Everything else follows.
