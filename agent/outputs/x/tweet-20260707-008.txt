SAP just made the boldest bet in enterprise software: AI agents run your operations end-to-end.

Not "AI-assisted workflows." Not "smarter dashboards." The whole thing.

At Sapphire 2026, they called it the "Autonomous Enterprise." They're arguing the ERP era is over — and agents are what replaces it.

The interesting part isn't the product. It's the framing.

SAP is telling CFOs and CIOs: the future isn't humans using better software. It's agents handling operational work while humans set the goals.

That's a massive shift in how enterprise software sells. For 40 years, the pitch was "make your people more productive." Now it's "reduce how many people you need for operations."

Bold claim. Big market. The question is whether agents can actually deliver on that promise at enterprise scale, with enterprise compliance requirements.

Two data points that say they might: Salesforce Agentforce closed 29,000 deals and hit $1.2B ARR. Iron Mountain dropped chat abandonment 70% with AI-only responses.

The pattern is starting to hold.
