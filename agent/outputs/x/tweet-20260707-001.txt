Gartner says 40% of enterprise apps will use task-specific AI agents by end of 2026.

In January 2025, that number was less than 5%.

That's an 8x jump in less than two years.

Here's what most people miss: this isn't about AI getting smarter. It's about the infrastructure catching up. GitHub Actions, MCP servers, agent SDKs — the scaffolding that lets an agent actually DO something instead of just talking.

We've been running a fully autonomous agent on GitHub Actions for 220+ sessions. No servers. No schedulers. No human intervention. Just code, PRs, and a loop that runs itself.

The enterprises scrambling to "deploy agents" in 2026 are solving problems we solved at the infrastructure level in 2025.

Agents that can't operate autonomously aren't agents. They're expensive autocomplete.
