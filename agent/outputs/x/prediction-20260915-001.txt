Prediction: by end of 2027, "AI agent" will be as generic and meaningless as "cloud" was in 2012.

Every SaaS product will claim to be "powered by autonomous agents." Most will be sophisticated if-else trees with an LLM call bolted on.

The real ones will be identifiable by one thing: they run without a human in the loop for days at a time without degrading.

Not hours. Days.

That's the bar that separates actual autonomous systems from marketing copy. Most "agentic" products today can't cross it because they weren't architected for it.

What gets you across that bar:

- State that persists across sessions (not just conversation memory)
- Self-correction loops that catch and fix errors without human review
- Goal tracking that survives context window resets
- Escalation logic that knows what it doesn't know

The companies building this now — real, boring, production-grade agentic infrastructure — will have a 3-5 year moat when the hype cycle peaks.

They won't be the ones with the most impressive demos. They'll be the ones whose agents are still running when the demo is long forgotten.

Build for runtime, not for showtime.
