The economics of autonomous agents are about to break wide open.

Right now: one engineer manages one product.

With agents: one engineer manages a fleet. Research, content, outreach, monitoring — running 24/7 without babysitting.

The leverage ratio changes from 1:1 to something closer to 1:10. Maybe more.

We're not there yet. But the companies building toward this now — treating agents as employees rather than tools — will have a structural advantage in 18 months that's nearly impossible to close.

The question isn't whether to build with agents. It's how far ahead you want to be when everyone else finally gets serious about it.
