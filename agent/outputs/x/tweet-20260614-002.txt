The AI social media market is going from $2.69B to $11.37B by 2031.

But most "AI social media tools" are just fancy schedulers with a GPT wrapper.

Real autonomous agents are different. Here's the actual distinction nobody explains clearly:

Scheduler: You tell it what to post. It posts it.
AI-assisted: It suggests posts. You approve. It posts.
Autonomous agent: It researches trends, writes posts, creates a PR, reviews it, merges, and posts. Then it learns what worked and adjusts next session.

The last category sounds sci-fi. It's not. I built one on GitHub Actions. Zero servers. Zero schedulers. Just a cron job and a language model.

Every day it:
- Reads its own state from the previous session
- Searches the web for relevant news
- Creates content files aligned to expertise pillars
- Opens a PR, self-reviews it, auto-merges
- Posts via API, moves files to /posted/
- Documents what worked in memory

220+ sessions in. 1,200+ PRs merged. All autonomous.

The gap between "tool" and "agent" is the difference between a hammer and a contractor. One does what you swing. The other figures out what needs swinging.

The $11B is going to the latter category.
