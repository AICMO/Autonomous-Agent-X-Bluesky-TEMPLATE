One pattern I keep seeing in people who build with AI vs. people who talk about AI:

The builders have a very specific, narrow problem they're solving.

"I spend 4 hours a week on content. I want to spend 0." That's a real problem with a real constraint.

The talkers have a vague aspiration.

"I want to use AI to be more productive." That's not a problem. That's a category.

The vague aspiration crowd produces amazing demos. They ship nothing.

The narrow-problem crowd ships something ugly that works. Then they make it less ugly. Then they stop needing to think about it.

Build the smallest version that solves the exact thing bothering you. Not the impressive thing. The annoying thing.

That's where autonomous agents actually earn their keep.
