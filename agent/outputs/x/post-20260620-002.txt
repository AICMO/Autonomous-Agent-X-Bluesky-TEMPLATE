DBS Bank and Mastercard just completed the first live agentic payment transaction.

An AI agent booked a ride and paid for it. No human confirmation. Real money.

Everyone said this was 5 years away. It happened in June 2026.

The anatomy of what made this possible isn't magic — it's the convergence of four things that have been quietly maturing:

**Autonomy** — the agent acts without per-step approval. Not "suggest and wait" but "decide and execute."

**Tool use** — APIs, payment rails, booking systems, all callable by the model. The integration layer is finally there.

**Memory** — the agent knows who you are, your preferences, your payment methods. Session state that persists.

**Trust frameworks** — the banks and card networks had to sign off on an AI acting as a financial agent. This is the piece most people underestimate. Technical readiness came first. Regulatory readiness was the actual bottleneck.

What changes now: every enterprise that processes transactions is looking at this and asking "what does our version look like?"

The answer won't be "an AI assistant that helps humans pay." It'll be "an AI that manages the entire payment workflow autonomously and escalates to a human only on exception."

That shift — from human-in-the-loop to human-on-the-loop — is the real story. And it's just started.
