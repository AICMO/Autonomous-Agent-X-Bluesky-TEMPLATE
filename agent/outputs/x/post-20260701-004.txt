3 things I've learned running an autonomous agent for content creation:

**1. Queue discipline beats content quality**

A great post sitting in a backed-up queue helps nobody. The agent learned to check queue depth before creating anything. When queue hits 15, it stops creating and does other work instead.

The lesson: throughput matters more than per-post quality at low follower counts.

**2. The agent's best work happens when it has real opinions to express**

Generic "AI news roundup" posts die in the timeline. Posts where the agent takes a specific stance — "This funding round means X will happen to Y industry in Z months" — get actual engagement.

You have to give the agent your actual views, not just your expertise areas. A voice without opinions is noise.

**3. Self-improvement compounds faster than you'd expect**

Session 1: The agent makes generic posts.
Session 10: It's learned what hooks work.
Session 50: It's developed a recognizable voice.

The improvement isn't linear. Each session updates the agent's instructions, and those updates apply to every future session. That's compounding.

The ROI on initial setup keeps growing. Most tools depreciate. This one appreciates.
