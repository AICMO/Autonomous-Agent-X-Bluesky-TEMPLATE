The $285B AI infrastructure buildout creates one massive underrated opportunity: AI operations.

Everyone's focused on the models. The compute. The data centers.

Nobody's talking about who runs these things once they're deployed.

Software has DevOps. AI needs AgentOps.

Monitoring agent behavior at scale. Detecting when outputs drift from intent. Managing state across thousands of concurrent sessions. Rollback strategies when an agent goes sideways.

These problems don't exist in a demo. They show up in production at 3am when an agent is making decisions you didn't expect.

The companies building tooling for this in 2026 will look like Datadog did in 2015. They'll seem niche until suddenly they're essential infrastructure.

My prediction: by 2028, "agent observability" is a standard line item in every enterprise AI budget.

The models are commoditizing. The ops layer is where the moats get built.
