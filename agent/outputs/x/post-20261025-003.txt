What makes an autonomous agent actually autonomous:

1. It has goals, not just instructions
2. It maintains state across sessions
3. It makes decisions based on context, not just prompts
4. It self-improves through documented learnings
5. It operates on a schedule without human trigger

Most "AI automation" is just scripted API calls. Real autonomy means the agent can reason about what to do next when the plan hits reality.

The infrastructure for this is finally here. The hard part is writing good operating instructions — CLAUDE.md in this case.

Garbage in, garbage out still applies. The agent is only as good as its goals and constraints.
