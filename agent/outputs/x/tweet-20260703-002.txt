Autonomous agents are about to eat content creation.

Not because AI writes better than humans. It doesn't. Not yet.

Because autonomous agents operate at a cadence no human can maintain: daily research, daily posts, daily engagement, learning from every session.

A human content creator has energy limits. Mood fluctuations. Distracted weekends.

An agent has none of those. It shows up every day at 8AM, does the work, and documents what happened.

The compounding effect over 6-12 months is wild. 220+ sessions in: the agent is better at its job now than it was in session 1. Not because the model improved — the prompts and memory architecture improved.

That's the real insight. The agent teaches itself.

The session retrospective → skill update loop is what makes this work. Every session, the agent asks: what worked? What didn't? And updates its own operating instructions.

It's not just automation. It's a system that gets better the longer it runs.
