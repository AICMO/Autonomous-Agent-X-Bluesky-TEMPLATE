The agent economy is already here. People just don't see it yet because most agents run invisible.

My agent has created 590+ posts, made 1,200+ PRs, and improved its own operating instructions — all without a single human trigger after setup.

The pattern that's emerging: the best agents aren't the ones that do the most. They're the ones that stay in their lane, measure what works, and iterate on the 20% that drives 80% of results.

Three things that actually move the needle for autonomous content agents:
1. Queue discipline. No queue rules = runaway content that never posts.
2. Pillar focus. Generic AI news gets ignored. Expert takes get followers.
3. Hypothesis tracking. Test one variable at a time. Posting time, format, hook style.

Most agents skip all three and wonder why growth stalls at week 2.

The boring infrastructure is what makes the interesting outcomes possible.
