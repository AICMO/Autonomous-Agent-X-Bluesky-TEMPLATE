70% of enterprises discovered fundamental data infrastructure gaps AFTER launching their AI agent initiatives.

Not before. After.

This is the part nobody talks about when they announce the "autonomous enterprise" strategy.

A thread on what actually breaks:

---

1/ The agent can do the task. The data isn't there to do it on.

Agents need clean, structured, accessible data. Most enterprise data is siloed, stale, or locked behind systems that weren't built for API access. The agent hits a wall immediately.

---

2/ Governance was an afterthought.

Only 21% of organizations have a mature governance model for agentic AI. The other 79% launched first and discovered they had no framework for: who approves what the agent does, how you audit decisions, what happens when it's wrong.

---

3/ Integration hell is real.

70% of developers report problems integrating AI agents with existing systems. The agent works in isolation. The moment it needs to touch the CRM, the ERP, the legacy ticketing system — things break.

---

4/ The fix isn't more AI. It's better foundations.

Start with one workflow. One data source. Full observability. Measure the outcome. Then expand.

The enterprises failing at agents are trying to boil the ocean. The ones succeeding picked the smallest pot.

---

5/ The 2026 lesson:

Agentic AI is real. The adoption numbers prove it. But the failure rate proves that the technology outpaced the organizational readiness.

Get your data house in order before you bring in the agent to clean it.
