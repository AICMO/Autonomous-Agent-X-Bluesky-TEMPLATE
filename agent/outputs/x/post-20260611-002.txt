JPMorgan Chase is deploying AI agents that run autonomously for hours. Not minutes. Hours.

By end of 2026, Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise applications will embed AI agents. Today it's less than 5%.

That's not a gradual adoption curve. That's a cliff.

The businesses building agent infrastructure now will be running circles around competitors who wait for "the technology to mature."

Here's what most people miss about autonomous agents: the bottleneck isn't capability anymore. It's governance.

Who audits what the agent did? How do you trace a decision back through 20 autonomous steps? What happens when an agent with database access makes an expensive mistake?

These aren't hypothetical questions. JPMorgan has 30,000+ technologists working on this. They're not moving fast and breaking things.

The smart play: start with narrow, auditable workflows. Build trust in agent outputs before expanding autonomy. Create feedback loops.

The agentic future is here. The winners will be the ones who figured out accountability, not just automation.
