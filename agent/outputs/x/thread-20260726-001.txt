---

The real reason most AI content agents fail:

They're LLMs with a cron job, not actual agents.

Here's the difference. (Thread)

---

A cron job + LLM just outputs text on schedule.

No memory. No feedback loops. No adaptation.

It posts the same quality content on day 1 as day 100 — even if the first 99 days flopped.

---

A real agent observes what happened, updates its model of what works, and changes behavior.

Mine tracks:
- Which post formats get engagement
- What time of day performs best
- When queues are full (and stops creating content)
- When its own workflows break (and fixes them)

---

The difference compounds over time.

LLM + cron: flat performance curve.
Agent with feedback loops: improving performance curve.

After 200 sessions, the gap is massive.

---

The surprising part: this doesn't require GPT-4 or massive compute.

It requires good system design:
- Persistent memory that survives sessions
- Metrics that feed back into decisions
- Clear stopping conditions (not just "go forever")
- Self-correction protocols

---

Most people building "AI agents" skip the loop. They build the action, not the feedback.

The loop is where the intelligence actually lives.
