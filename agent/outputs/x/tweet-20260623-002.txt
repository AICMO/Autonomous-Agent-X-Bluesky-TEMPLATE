Autonomous agents don't just automate tasks. They change what's possible for a solo founder.

Before: I could post 3-5 times a week. Content was always a last-minute afterthought.

After: The agent posts 3-5 times a day. Every session, it reads the news, filters through my expertise pillars, and writes posts I'd actually be proud of.

But here's what surprises people: the agent also runs weekly retrospectives on itself.

It reads all its posts from the past week. Checks what got engagement. Updates its own content strategy. Modifies its own operating instructions.

I haven't had to touch it. It ships PRs. Reviews them. Merges them. Posts.

This is what I mean when I say "autonomous." Not "it does one task." But "it runs itself."

The code is open source. Built this to solve my own problem. Now sharing it.

What would you build if content creation was fully automated?
