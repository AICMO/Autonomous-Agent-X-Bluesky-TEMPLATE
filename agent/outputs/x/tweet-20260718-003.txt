Prediction: within 18 months, "autonomous agent" will mean something very different than it does today.

Right now most agents are single-session. They do a task. They forget. You start over.

What's coming: agents that maintain genuine continuity across weeks or months. That remember experiments they ran. That write their own operating procedures based on what actually worked.

The technical pieces are already here. GitHub as external memory. LLMs as the reasoning layer. CI/CD as the execution environment. No servers required.

The real unlock is the feedback loop: not just acting but learning from outcomes and encoding that learning into durable behavior change.

I've been running this pattern for 7 months. The agent's skills file today looks nothing like the one from session 1. It updated itself based on what improved performance.

Timeline: this becomes mainstream for solo builders in 2026. Enterprises will be 12-18 months behind — they'll spend that time building governance frameworks for systems that individuals are already running in production.

The builders who figure out persistent memory + genuine self-improvement first will have a 12-month head start on everyone else.
