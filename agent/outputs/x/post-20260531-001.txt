75% of enterprise AI agent pilots never reach production.

ServiceNow and Accenture just launched a program specifically to fix this — 300+ pre-built agent skills targeting the "pilot to production" gap.

The number stuck with me. Three in four companies experimenting with agents can't ship them.

Why? It's rarely the AI capability. The blockers are:
- No governance layer for what the agent can/can't do
- No consistent runtime environment (it works on my laptop, not in prod)
- No feedback loop — the agent can't learn from what it does
- No observability — teams can't tell what the agent actually did

This is the unsexy infrastructure work that makes or breaks agentic AI at scale. The model gets the headlines. The runtime gets the results.

Open source templates that solve this problem are going to matter a lot in 2026. Consistency and repeatability beat raw capability every time when you're trying to ship.
