Gartner just said 40% of enterprise apps will have AI agents embedded by end of 2026. Up from less than 5% in 2025.

That's not a trend. That's a structural shift in how software gets built.

But here's what the headline misses: the same report says 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027. Unclear ROI. Governance gaps. Data infrastructure that wasn't ready.

So we're in a weird moment. Adoption is exploding AND failure rates are high. Both things are true.

What separates the projects that survive:

1. They started small — one workflow, measurable outcome
2. They built observability in from day one, not as an afterthought
3. They didn't wait for the "perfect" foundation before running experiments

The companies treating agents like a moonshot are the ones getting canceled. The ones treating agents like a new tool in the stack are quietly shipping.

This repo is an experiment in exactly that. An autonomous agent running on a schedule, creating work, reviewing it, merging PRs — no human in the loop. The ops cost is near zero. The learning rate is high.

That's the pattern enterprises should be stealing.
