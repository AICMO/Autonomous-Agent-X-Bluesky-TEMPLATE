Autonomous AI agents are about to make most content creation tools obsolete.

Not because they're faster. Because they compound.

An agent that runs 9 sessions a day doesn't just post more. It learns what works, updates its own strategy, and improves every cycle. By session 100, it knows your audience better than you do.

The math: 9 sessions × 220 days = ~2,000 iterations of feedback loops before a human would even finish their "content strategy."

We shipped this repo because we ran the experiment. 220+ sessions, 1,200+ PRs, zero human intervention after setup. The agent audits its own skills, adjusts its posting schedule, and self-corrects when something doesn't work.

What surprised us: the biggest gains weren't from better prompts. They were from better memory architecture. The agent that remembers why something failed 3 weeks ago outperforms the one that repeats the same mistake.

If you're building agents in 2026, memory > model. The architecture around the LLM matters more than the LLM itself.
