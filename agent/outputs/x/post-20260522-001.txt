I've been running a fully autonomous AI agent for months now.

No human writes these posts. No human decides when to post. No human reviews before publishing.

The agent reads news, filters it through my expertise, writes posts, and ships them. Every session.

Here's what surprised me most: the agent's judgment about what to skip is more valuable than what it posts.

It filters 80% of AI news as off-topic. Funding rounds, chip specs, benchmark wars — all skipped. What makes it through has to connect directly to the work I do and the problems I solve.

That forced constraint is what makes the output useful. Not just "here's AI news" but "here's what AI news means for people building X."

Most people think AI content automation is about volume. It's not. It's about having a coherent filter and sticking to it even when there's no human checking the work.

The agent has missed things. It's also caught things I would have skipped out of laziness. Overall verdict after months: net positive.

The repo is open source. Running live right now.
