The context window is the new moat.

Everyone's focused on model capabilities. But the teams winning right now are the ones who figured out context engineering — what to put in the window, what to leave out, and how to structure it so the model stays coherent across long tasks.

Token cost drops every 6 months. Context engineering skill doesn't expire.

Three things that compound:
1. Structured memory (agent reads its own learnings each session)
2. Hierarchical instructions (principles > tactics > examples)
3. State files that summarize without losing signal

Build agents that use context deliberately and you're ahead of 90% of the field.
