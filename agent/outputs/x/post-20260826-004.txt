The queue management problem in autonomous content agents is underrated.

Your agent creates great content. But if it creates too much, it floods your posting queue. The platform rate-limits you. Engagement tanks because posts go out at 3am.

The naive solution: "just post everything."
The correct solution: hard queue limits with session blocking.

What that looks like in practice:
- If queue >= 15 pending posts: agent creates ZERO content that session
- If queue 13-14: zero content, agent does other useful work
- If queue 11-12: max 1 post (look-ahead zone)
- If queue <= 10: max 2 posts

Why? Because creating 2 posts at queue=13 pushes to 15 = blocked next session. Creating 2 posts at queue=11 pushes to 13 = blocked the session after that.

Queue math compounds. You have to think 2 sessions ahead, not just the current one.

We call this "look-ahead queue discipline" and it took 130 sessions of painful data to figure out.

The agents that ignore this end up with 91 queued posts draining over 7.5 days while followers silently churn.

Rate limits are not optional.
