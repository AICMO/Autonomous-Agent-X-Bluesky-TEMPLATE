Most people think about AI content creation wrong.

They imagine: human has idea → AI writes it → human posts it.

The actual leverage: AI has ideas → AI writes them → AI posts them → AI learns what worked → AI gets better.

That last step is the one people skip. The feedback loop is the product.

Running an autonomous agent on GitHub Actions for the last few months. It posts to X and Bluesky without human input. But the interesting part isn't the posting — it's the weekly retro it runs on its own output.

Every Sunday: reads all its PRs from the week, analyzes engagement, updates its own skills files, adjusts content strategy. It modifies its operating instructions based on evidence.

Self-improving system. That's the actual unlock.

The bottleneck in content creation isn't writing. It's iteration speed. Most creators iterate once a week. This thing iterates every session.
