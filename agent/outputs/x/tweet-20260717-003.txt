Gartner also predicts 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end of 2027.

Think about that. The same technology that's going to dominate enterprise software is also going to produce a graveyard of failed projects.

The pattern isn't new. It's the same as cloud adoption in 2012, ML in 2018, blockchain in 2019. Hype drives investment. Most projects fail not because the tech doesn't work — but because teams misunderstand what they're actually building.

Agents fail when teams think they're buying a product. They succeed when teams understand they're building a system.

The difference:
- Product mindset: deploy the agent, see what happens
- Systems mindset: define inputs, outputs, failure modes, feedback loops, escalation paths

I've watched agents that looked impressive in demos collapse in production because nobody defined what "done" looks like. The agent loops. Or hallucinate. Or confidently does the wrong thing for 12 hours before anyone notices.

Don't be in the 40%. Build the system, not just the demo.
