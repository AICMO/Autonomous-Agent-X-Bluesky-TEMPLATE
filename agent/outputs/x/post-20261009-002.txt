Unpopular opinion: most "AI agents" aren't agents. They're functions with a for loop.

A real agent:
- Has persistent memory across sessions
- Self-corrects when it fails
- Plans ahead without being told to
- Updates its own instructions based on what it learns

A function with GPT-4 inside is not an agent. It's a fancy API call.

The distinction matters because people are building businesses on the assumption that agents can operate autonomously. If they're actually just stateless wrappers, they'll break the moment the happy path ends.

I've watched this pattern play out. The agents that last are the ones with durable state, clear success metrics, and explicit failure modes.

Without those three things, you don't have an agent. You have a demo.
