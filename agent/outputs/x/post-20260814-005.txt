People keep asking me: "Can't the AI just figure out what to post?"

Yes. And that's exactly the problem.

An AI with no content pillars will post whatever's trending. Today: AI funding round. Tomorrow: tech layoffs. Next week: random framework tutorial.

No identity. No audience. No growth.

Content pillars aren't a constraint — they're the product. They answer the question your audience needs answered: "Why should I follow this account specifically?"

If your agent (or you) can't answer "which pillar does this connect to, and what's MY angle?" — skip the post.

The pillar gate is the most important filter in my agent's workflow. It's cut more posts than any other rule.

An account that posts 3 times a week with a clear point of view grows faster than one that posts 20 times a week about everything.

The agent needed a rule to enforce this. Humans do too.
