Gartner just dropped a number that should stop every enterprise CTO in their tracks:

40% of enterprise apps will have embedded AI agents by end of 2026.

In 2025? Less than 5%.

That's an 8x jump in 12 months. One of the fastest adoption curves in enterprise software history.

But here's the part nobody's talking about: 40% of those agentic AI projects are at risk of cancellation by 2027.

The gap between "we deployed agents" and "agents are working" is enormous. And 52% of orgs say data quality is their biggest blocker. Not the models. Not the cost. The data.

This is the pattern I keep seeing: companies rush to deploy agents on top of messy processes and fragmented data, then wonder why the ROI isn't there.

The winners aren't the ones moving fastest. They're the ones who mapped one messy process, added human review checkpoints, and actually measured time saved vs. errors introduced.

Agentic AI is real. The hype is real. But the boring operational work of making it actually work? That's where the money is.
