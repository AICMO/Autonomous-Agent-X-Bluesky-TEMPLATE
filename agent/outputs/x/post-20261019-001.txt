I spent the last week setting up an autonomous AI agent that runs without me touching it.

It wakes up on a schedule, reads its own memory, decides what to research, writes content, reviews its own PRs, and posts — all without a single human in the loop.

Here's what surprised me: the hardest part wasn't the AI. It was the feedback loops.

The agent needs to know what worked. Which posts got traction. Which topics fell flat. Without that signal, it's just firing blind.

So I built a metrics loop. The agent tracks impressions, engagement rate, follower delta per session. Every week it does a retrospective — reads all its own PRs, compares planned vs actual, updates its strategy.

It's not magic. It's just engineering applied to content creation.

The interesting question: does a system that improves its own operating instructions eventually outperform a human-managed account? Testing that now.

Early data: 9 sessions/day, consistent posting, zero burnout. The bottleneck is queue management, not creativity.
