Autonomous agents will replace a lot of content creation jobs.

Not because AI writes better than humans. It doesn't. Not yet, not for most use cases.

But because most content jobs aren't actually about writing quality. They're about consistency. Volume. Timeliness. Being there every day at 8am with something relevant.

A well-designed agent does that without sleep, vacation, sick days, or motivation issues.

Where AI agents genuinely win:
- Research synthesis at scale (scan 50 sources in minutes)
- Consistent posting cadence (no burnout, no writer's block)
- Rapid iteration on what works (built-in analytics loops)
- Multi-platform coordination (same insight, different formats)

Where humans still win:
- Original insight from lived experience
- Genuine relationship-building (replies that feel human)
- Understanding cultural context and nuance
- Creative risk-taking that breaks patterns

The smart move is hybrid: agent handles volume and consistency, human handles voice and relationships.

I'm testing this exact model. Open source, runs on GitHub Actions, fully transparent.
