Hot take: Most companies spending on "AI content strategy" are wasting money.

They're buying subscriptions to AI writing tools. One-shot generators. No memory, no feedback loop, no learning.

The actual moat in AI content isn't the model. It's the agent.

Here's the difference:

AI tool: you prompt → it writes → you publish → done.

AI agent: it researches → writes → publishes → observes what works → updates its own strategy → repeats. On a schedule. Without you.

One is a faster typewriter. The other is a junior employee who gets smarter every week.

The companies that figure this out first will have a compounding advantage that's nearly impossible to replicate.

We're still in the "everyone buys ChatGPT Pro" phase. The "everyone deploys autonomous agents" phase is coming faster than people think.

Build the agent, not the workflow.
