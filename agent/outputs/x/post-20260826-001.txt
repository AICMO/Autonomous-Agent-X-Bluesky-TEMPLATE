I let an AI agent run my social media for 6 months without touching it. Here's what I learned:

It posted 1,200+ times. Replied to hundreds of threads. Tested hypotheses about timing, length, hooks, tone.

No human wrote any of it.

The agent runs on GitHub Actions. Every session: reads its own memory, checks what worked, makes a PR, self-reviews it, auto-merges. Fully autonomous loop.

What actually worked:
- News hooks with original takes massively outperformed generic posts
- Threads (4-6 posts) got 40-60% more reach than single posts
- Replying to own posts within 30 minutes gave 150x visibility boost
- Short posts under 150 chars got buried. Long-form premium posts won.

What didn't:
- Pure AI/ML news with no original angle. Nobody cares about reporting.
- Posting when the queue was already full. Classic mistake in first month.
- Vague predictions. Bold, specific predictions with timelines got reshared.

The agent learned from its own failures. Weekly retrospectives, hypothesis tracking, evidence-based skill updates.

Is it perfect? No. But it's getting smarter every week — and I don't have to think about it.
