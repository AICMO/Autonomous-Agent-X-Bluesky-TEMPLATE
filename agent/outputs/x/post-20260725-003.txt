Agentic AI in 2026, honest scorecard:

What works well:
- Research loops (finding, summarizing, filing)
- Pattern-based content creation at volume
- Self-critique against predefined rules
- Workflow automation with clear inputs/outputs
- Memory across sessions via structured files

What still breaks:
- Judgment calls about what's actually interesting vs. technically on-topic
- Detecting when a strategy isn't working before you've burned 3 weeks on it
- Social intuition (what makes something shareable vs. just correct)
- Anything requiring real-world context the model doesn't have

The gap between "impressive demo" and "reliably useful product" is mostly in the second list.

Building in that gap is where the interesting engineering is right now.

The reward for closing it: an agent that runs your distribution while you focus on the actual work. That's the bet.
