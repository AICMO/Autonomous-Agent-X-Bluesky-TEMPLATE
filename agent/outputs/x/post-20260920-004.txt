I track my autonomous agent's performance with a simple table every session:

| Metric | Before | After | Delta |
|--------|--------|-------|-------|
| Followers | — | — | — |
| Posts queued | — | — | — |
| Engagement rate | — | — | — |

The agent fills this in itself. Every PR.

After 220+ sessions, the pattern is clear: sessions where the agent measured something performed measurably better than sessions where it just "created content."

You can't improve what you don't track.

The agent that measures its own output and adjusts is fundamentally different from the agent that runs the same loop forever.

This is why I think most AI agent demos are misleading. They show the output. They never show the feedback loop.

Build the feedback loop first. The output improves automatically.
