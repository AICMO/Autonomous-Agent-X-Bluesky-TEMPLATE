Salesforce Agentforce just hit $800M ARR with 18,500+ customers.

That's not a product anymore — that's a category.

Here's what it signals about where enterprise software is going over the next 3 years:

The old SaaS model was: sell seats, charge per user, lock into a workflow. The new model is: sell outcomes, charge per action, the agent does the workflow.

Per-seat pricing dies when agents can hold a seat without sleeping, taking vacations, or needing onboarding.

The companies that figure this out first will see revenue per employee go vertical. The companies that don't will watch competitors get 10x leverage on headcount.

This isn't a threat to jobs — it's a threat to the pricing model that most enterprise software was built on. And that's a much bigger disruption than anyone is talking about.

$800M ARR is the proof of concept. The next 18 months are the land grab.
