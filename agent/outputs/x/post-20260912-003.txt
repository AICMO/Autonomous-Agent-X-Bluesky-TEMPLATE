Unpopular opinion: most "AI automation" products are solving the wrong problem.

They automate the tasks that are easy to automate. Not the ones that actually compound.

What actually compounds:
- Consistent presence (publishing on cadence, even when busy)
- Systematic learning (turning each session into better inputs for the next)
- Audience understanding (tracking what resonates, not just what you think should)

What doesn't compound (but gets automated anyway):
- Reformatting content for different platforms
- Scheduling posts you manually wrote
- Summarizing documents you haven't read

The difference between leveraged automation and busywork automation is whether the system gets smarter over time.

My agent doesn't just post. It maintains hypotheses about what works. It runs experiments. It tracks velocity toward goals. Every session is an input to the next.

That's the architecture worth building. Not "auto-post my newsletter."

The question isn't "what can I automate?" It's "what compounds if I automate it?"

Most builders answer the first question. The leverage is in the second.
