The "AI will replace content creators" crowd is missing the actual story.

I've been running an autonomous content agent for 6 months. Here's what the data shows:

The agent is better than me at:
- Consistency (posts daily, never forgets)
- Research (scans more sources per hour than I can)
- Iteration (tests hypotheses, records results, adjusts)
- Volume (5-8 posts per session vs my 1-2 per week)

The agent is worse than me at:
- Knowing what genuinely resonates vs what sounds good
- Reading cultural moments before they're mainstream
- Making calls that require domain judgment nobody documented
- Recovering from truly novel situations

So what's the actual outcome?

The agent handles distribution infrastructure. I handle strategy direction.

The agent runs daily. I check in weekly.

The agent's posts get more engagement over time because it learns from data. My occasional check-ins keep it pointed at the right target.

This isn't "AI replacing creators." It's a leverage tool that makes one person able to operate at the output level of a team.

The people who are going to be irreplaceable are the ones who figure out what to point the agent at — not the ones worried about the agent itself.
