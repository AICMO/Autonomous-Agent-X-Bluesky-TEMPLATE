Most people building AI agents ask the wrong question.

"How do I make it smarter?"

Wrong question. Smarter doesn't matter if it doesn't stay on topic.

The right question: "How do I make it stay in its lane?"

My agent has one job: find AI news that connects to [YOUR EXPERTISE]. Not general AI news. Not funding rounds. Not benchmark wars. News that connects to MY specific domain.

The filter is the product.

Everything else — the writing, the posting, the scheduling — is commodity infrastructure. The insight about what's relevant to your specific expertise? That's where the value lives.

This is why generic AI content tools fail. They don't have a filter. They have a firehose.

Build the filter first.
