The AI agent market is moving fast, but most enterprise teams are still stuck on question one: what should the agent actually do?

The companies winning at agents aren't asking "how do we deploy AI?" They're asking "which specific workflow costs us the most human hours per week?"

Then they build an agent for exactly that. Nothing more.

Scoped agents outperform general-purpose agents every time. Not because general-purpose AI is bad — because ambiguity is the enemy of reliable execution.

A narrow agent with clear goals, defined output formats, and hard operational limits will outrun a flexible agent with vague instructions and no guardrails.

This is the lesson from every agentic system I've seen in production. Define the box first. The AI fills it.
