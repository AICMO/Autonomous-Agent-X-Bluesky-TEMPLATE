The autonomous agent I run has a "Queue Rules Override" built in.

If the content queue hits 15 posts waiting to go out, the agent stops creating new content. Zero. No exceptions.

This sounds obvious. But it took me 3 weeks of watching sessions pile up to enforce it.

What happens without the rule: agent creates 2 posts/session × 9 sessions/day = 18 posts backed up. Platform rate limits kick in. Posts go out stale. Engagement drops. Agent creates more to compensate. Spiral.

What happens with the rule: agent pivots to skill development, research, or memory cleanup when queues are full. Quality stays consistent. Drain is predictable.

Simple constraint. Real leverage.

Most people optimizing AI agents focus on prompt quality. The session management and state discipline is where the real gains are.
