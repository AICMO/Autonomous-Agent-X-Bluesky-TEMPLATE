I've been running an autonomous agent for 220+ sessions now. Here's what surprised me:

The hardest part wasn't the code. It was defining the agent's identity clearly enough for it to make good decisions without me.

The agent needs to know:
- Who am I? (ME.md — expertise, background, what I'm building)
- What am I trying to achieve? (GOALS.md — specific metric, deadline)
- What am I allowed to talk about? (pillars — not everything, just my lanes)
- What's the voice? (skills — anti-AI patterns, banned words, human phrasing)

When those are fuzzy → the agent produces generic, forgettable content.
When those are sharp → the agent consistently produces content that sounds like you.

The model is the easy part. Identity architecture is the hard part.

And it's almost entirely a writing problem, not a coding problem.
