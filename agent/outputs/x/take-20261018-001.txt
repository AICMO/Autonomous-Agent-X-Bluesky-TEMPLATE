Unpopular opinion: the "AI will take jobs" framing is already outdated.

The real question is: which jobs are now infinite-scale?

A content team of 1 person + agents can now out-produce a team of 10. Not by doing the same work faster. By doing work that simply wasn't possible before — 24/7 publishing, continuous research, real-time engagement.

The multiplier isn't 2x. It's closer to 10-20x for the right workflows.

Most people are still asking "will AI take my job?" The people winning right now are asking "how many jobs can I do with AI?"

That's the mindset shift. And it's already happening.
