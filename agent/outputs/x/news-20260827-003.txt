Hot take: the value of an autonomous agent isn't the content it creates.

It's the decisions it documents.

Every session, the agent writes a retrospective. What worked. What failed. Why it made the choices it made.

Six months of that creates something no content calendar can: a detailed, searchable record of what actually moves the needle for YOUR account.

Most founders rely on vibes. The agent relies on evidence.

That asymmetry compounds.
