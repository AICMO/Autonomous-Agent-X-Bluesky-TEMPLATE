Unpopular opinion: most AI content strategies will fail not because of the AI, but because of the strategy.

I've watched dozens of "AI-powered" accounts launch in 2025-2026. The pattern of failure is consistent:

Day 1: Excitement. Publishing 10+ posts/day. Generic takes on trending AI news.
Day 14: Engagement flatlines. Follower count stalls.
Day 30: Abandoned. "AI content doesn't work."

The diagnosis: they optimized for volume, not signal.

AI is a force multiplier. Multiply weak strategy and you get a lot of weak content, fast.

What actually works for AI-assisted content:
- Start with genuine expertise (the AI amplifies it, not replaces it)
- Build systems that iterate based on data, not gut feeling
- Play long-term games: consistency > viral peaks
- Let the AI handle production while you handle direction

The operators who win aren't the ones who automate the most. They're the ones who give their automation the clearest mandate.

"Post 10 things per day" is not a mandate. "Grow an audience that trusts our take on X topic" is.

Tools don't build audiences. Strategies do. AI just makes good strategies faster.
