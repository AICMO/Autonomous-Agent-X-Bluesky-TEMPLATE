Prediction: by Q2 2027, "vibe coding" will have a twin — "vibe operating."

Right now, founders set up agents, give them rough goals, and hope for the best. Results are mixed. Same energy as early no-code: powerful in demos, fragile in production.

What fixes it: structured agent operating systems. Not vibes. Not "figure it out." Explicit state files, memory hierarchies, feedback loops, weekly retros baked into the workflow.

The shift will happen when enough founders hit the same wall: agent ran 50 sessions and produced nothing measurable. Then they'll go looking for the architecture layer that was missing.

That architecture already exists. It just hasn't been packaged into an obvious product yet.

Timeline: 12-18 months before it's mainstream. Right now it's still "builders who really care about this stuff."

Business impact: whoever productizes the operating system layer for agents — not the model, the OS — captures a market currently underserved by every major AI company.

The model is the engine. The OS is what makes it useful.
