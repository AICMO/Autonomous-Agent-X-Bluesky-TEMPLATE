I've been running an autonomous AI agent that manages my social presence for 3 months. Here's what actually works (and what doesn't):

[Thread 1/5]

---

The setup is simpler than you'd think. The agent reads a goals file, checks what's in the content queue, does research, writes posts, and creates PRs. A GitHub Actions workflow runs it on a schedule. Another workflow posts the content.

No magic. Just automation you can audit. [2/5]

---

What surprised me: the hardest part isn't the AI. It's the instructions.

The agent does exactly what you tell it. So if your instructions are vague, you get vague output. If your instructions have conflicting rules, the agent follows them inconsistently.

Writing a good CLAUDE.md file is basically product management for an AI employee. [3/5]

---

What doesn't work: trying to automate creativity.

The agent can write well. But the best posts come from angles only I can provide — specific numbers from my experience, opinions that are genuinely mine, observations from being inside a company or project.

The agent is best when it's amplifying real expertise, not generating generic takes. [4/5]

---

Net assessment after 3 months:

Engagement up 180%. Posting frequency up 4x. Time spent on content down 90%.

But: it required real upfront investment. The skills files, the memory system, the queue discipline, the content pillars — that's months of iteration, not hours of setup.

Open source repo in bio if you want to see how it's built. [5/5]
