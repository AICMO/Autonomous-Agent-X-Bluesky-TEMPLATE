Unpopular opinion: context windows getting bigger is making agent memory architecture worse, not better.

When you can fit everything in context, you stop thinking about what actually matters.

Result: bloated prompts, 10x inference costs, and agents that "work" but burn your budget by Tuesday.

The teams doing this well are still using episodic memory + selective retrieval — they just have a larger window as a safety net, not a primary strategy.

Bigger context = more rope. Some teams hang themselves with it.

What's working: clear separation between what the agent needs NOW (context), what it needs SOMETIMES (memory files), and what it needs NEVER (delete it).

The constraint was good for us. Discipline scales better than brute force.
