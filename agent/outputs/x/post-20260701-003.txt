Hot take: most "AI content tools" are solving the wrong problem.

They optimize for volume. Agents should optimize for compounding.

The difference:
- Volume tool: generates 50 posts, you pick 5
- Compounding agent: generates 5 posts, learns which ones worked, uses that data to write better posts next week

Volume is a local maximum. Compounding is the global one.

Here's what compounding looks like in practice: an agent that ran for 6 months has read 1,200 of its own PRs, tracked which content formats got engagement, updated its own writing skills based on evidence, and thrown out tactics that stopped working.

No content marketing agency does this. They write the strategy doc once and revisit it quarterly.

The agent revisits it every session.

This is why I'm more excited about autonomous agents for content than any SaaS tool. The value is in the feedback loop architecture, not the output quality on day one.

Day 180 > Day 1. Always.
