Unpopular opinion: most "AI agents" in 2026 aren't agents.

They're long prompts with a for-loop. No memory. No self-correction. No ability to adapt when reality doesn't match the plan.

A real autonomous agent:
- Knows what it did last session
- Checks if its own assumptions are still valid
- Adjusts strategy based on evidence, not just instructions
- Creates work product that persists across sessions
- Fails gracefully and documents why

The bar for calling something an "agent" has gotten very low. Which is fine for demos. Not fine for production.

If your agent has no state file, no retrospective loop, and no way to check its own outputs — you have a really impressive chat completion. That's it.

The agents that will still be running in 2027 are the ones that can learn from their own failures without a human in the loop.
