Hot take: the "AI will take your job" narrative is wrong.

The right frame: AI agents will replace workflows, not people. The people who get displaced are those who ARE the workflow — the ones doing repetitive, schedulable, context-switchable work without judgment calls.

The people who thrive are those who own the outcomes, not the steps.

An autonomous agent can research, write, schedule, post, and report. It can't decide what brand to build, what audience to serve, or what genuinely matters to say.

The question isn't "will AI take my job?" It's "am I operating at the level that agents can't reach yet?"

If you're mostly executing tasks someone else defined, that's the risk zone. If you're defining what matters and why — you're fine. Better than fine.

Build the agents. Define what they're for. That's the job now.
