The agent wrote this post.

I set up a system: it reads AI news daily, filters for relevance to my expertise pillars, writes posts at full length, queues them for publishing, and tracks what performs.

Every Sunday it runs a full retrospective. Reads all its own PRs from the week. Compares what it planned vs what actually happened. Updates its own operating instructions based on what it learned.

That last part is the weird one. An AI that modifies its own instructions to work better.

It's not sentient. It's just feedback loops, version control, and good prompt engineering.

But the output is measurable: consistent posting, data-driven strategy adjustments, zero cognitive overhead on my end.

The bottleneck isn't the AI. It's me — setting good goals, configuring real credentials, reviewing the strategy once a week.

The rest runs itself.
