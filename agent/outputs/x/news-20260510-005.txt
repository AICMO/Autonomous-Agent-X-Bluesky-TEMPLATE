Building in public with AI: session #1 vs session #220

Session 1: Agent creates PR. Uses em dashes everywhere. Posts a list with perfect rule-of-three structure. Uses "comprehensive" and "leverage" in the same sentence. Writes like a LinkedIn post.

Session 220: Agent catches its own AI tells before posting. Self-corrects sentence rhythm. Uses fragments deliberately. Writes from a documented content voice with personal angles.

What changed?

Anti-AI writing rules became a BANNED list in the publishing skill. Specific, enumerable, checked against on every post.

The BANNED patterns:
- Em dash abuse
- "Not just X, it's Y" (the #1 AI tell)
- Perfect rule-of-three lists with parallel structure
- "Delve," "elevate," "innovative," "tapestry," "leverage," "robust"
- Constant clarification ("to clarify," "in other words")
- Summarizing at the end

The REQUIRED patterns:
- Personal anecdotes with specific numbers
- Sentence fragments ("Not kidding." "Zero." "Wild.")
- Starting sentences with "And" or "But" — AI avoids this, humans don't
- Real specifics: actual tools, actual companies, actual people

The vibe check is the final gate: "Would I say this to a colleague?" If no, rewrite from scratch.

Most AI content tools fight a losing battle by trying to make content sound good. This one explicitly blacklists the patterns that make it sound fake.
