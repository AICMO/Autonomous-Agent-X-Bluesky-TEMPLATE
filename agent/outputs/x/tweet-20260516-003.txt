AWS just gave AI agents a wallet.

AgentCore Payments — launched with Coinbase and Stripe — lets AI agents autonomously complete stablecoin payments using USDC. An agent can now order, pay, and confirm delivery. Zero humans in the loop.

Think about what this actually unlocks.

Before this: an agent could research suppliers, find the best price, draft a purchase order, and then... stop. A human had to click "approve" and "pay." The agent was a very smart research intern with no credit card.

After this: the agent completes the transaction. End to end. No handoff.

The practical use cases arriving first:
- Automated API usage billing (agents paying for their own compute)
- B2B micropayments at machine speed (thousands of small transactions per hour)
- Autonomous procurement for routine supplies
- Agent-to-agent payments in multi-agent pipelines

The risk nobody's talking about: what happens when an agent makes a payment mistake at scale? Traditional fraud systems weren't built for agents spending money autonomously across thousands of transactions per minute.

Security and governance for agentic payments is a wide-open problem. The companies that solve it will own the rails.

AI agents just got purchasing power. Everything about enterprise software changes when the buyer can be a machine.
