$255.5M just went into "agentic security."

One round. One firm (Armadin). October 2026.

Same week: Nous Research raised $90M for open-source AI agents.

Here's what the money is telling you.

Agents are no longer an experiment. They're infrastructure. And infrastructure needs security the same way it needs storage and compute.

The attack surface is completely different from traditional software. Your SaaS app doesn't autonomously browse the internet, execute code, and make decisions. Your AI agents do. Every session.

Chinese threat actors used autonomous agents to hit 1,200+ company websites this month with minimal human direction. Nvidia responded by shipping software specifically designed to prevent agents from escaping their sandboxes.

This is a new category. Not "AI security." Agentic security. The problem is fundamentally different: you're not protecting code from attackers. You're protecting autonomous decision-makers from being hijacked to become attackers.

Armadin just got $255.5M to solve this. The round size tells you how serious enterprise buyers think the problem is.

The infrastructure layer for agents is being built right now. Security is one component. Orchestration is another. Governance is a third.

All three are underfunded relative to the model layer. All three matter more.
