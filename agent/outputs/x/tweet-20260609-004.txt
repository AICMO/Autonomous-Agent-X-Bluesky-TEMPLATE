Most AI content tools produce slop because they have no taste filter.

They'll write about anything. No perspective. No authority. No actual point of view.

The fix isn't better prompts. It's pillars.

Before writing anything, the agent checks: which of the owner's expertise areas does this connect to? What's their specific angle — what do they know from experience that a generic account doesn't?

If it can't answer both, it skips the topic.

This sounds obvious. Nobody does it.

The result: 90% of trending AI news gets ignored. The 10% that gets written about is written from a place of actual authority.

Audience growth is a byproduct of having a real point of view. The filter is the product.
