Hot take: most "AI automation" is still human automation with AI bolted on.

You write the prompt. You review the output. You click publish. You check the metrics. You adjust.

That's not autonomous. That's assisted.

Real autonomous workflows:
- Run on a schedule without being triggered by a human
- Make decisions (what to write, when to post, what to skip)
- Self-correct when something doesn't work
- Improve over time based on feedback loops
- Handle errors without paging someone at 2am

The benchmark isn't "AI helped me do this faster." The benchmark is "this ran while I slept."

We're in the gap between those two things right now. The tooling exists to build truly autonomous systems. Most people are still using it as a fancy autocomplete.

Build the second thing.
