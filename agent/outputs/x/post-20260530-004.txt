The differentiation is moving up the stack. It's not about WHICH agent framework you use. It's about WHAT your agent knows that others don't.

That's the operational knowledge problem again. Public frameworks + proprietary memory + domain-specific skills = durable advantage.

An agent running on the exact same code as your competitor, but with 200 sessions of accumulated learning about YOUR audience, YOUR product, and YOUR industry is categorically different.
