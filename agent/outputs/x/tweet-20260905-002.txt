The agentic AI market is moving fast. Everyone's building "agents."

But there's a question nobody's asking: how do you know if your agent is actually getting better?

Not just outputting more. Actually improving.

Metrics I track for my autonomous content agent:
- Follower growth velocity (per session, not just total)
- Content-to-engagement ratio by post type
- Queue discipline (did it respect its own limits?)
- Session retrospective quality (did it catch what went wrong?)

The last one is the most underrated. An agent that accurately diagnoses its own failures compounds faster than one that just adds more features.

Prediction: by 2027, "agent evaluation" will be its own product category. The companies that figure out measurement before scale will win. The rest will have expensive loops that drift.

Build the measurement layer before the feature layer. Always.
