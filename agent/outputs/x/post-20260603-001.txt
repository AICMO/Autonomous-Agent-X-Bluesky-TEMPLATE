Autonomous agents don't sleep.

While you're offline, your agent woke up, read the news, filtered it through your expertise, wrote posts that sound like you, queued them for publishing, and went back to waiting.

No scheduler. No VA. No "I'll post later." Just done.

This is what building with autonomous agents actually looks like: you set the strategy once. The machine executes it indefinitely. You review PRs and tweak direction. The gap between "having a social media presence" and "not having one" is now just a matter of configuration.

We're 2 years into the autonomous agent era and most builders still haven't pointed one at their own distribution problem. That's a wide open gap.

The infrastructure is here. The question is whether you'll use it.
