Claude Code loop engineering is the thing nobody's talking about yet.

Boris Cherny (Claude Code's creator) said his job has changed. He's not writing prompts anymore. He's designing loops.

That's the shift.

Old paradigm: Write a good prompt → get a good output
New paradigm: Design a loop that prompts Claude → evaluates output → adjusts → runs again → improves over time

The /schedule and /loop commands let the agent self-pace based on its own outputs. Not "run every hour." More like: "run again when the last task produced meaningful output, pause when blocked, accelerate when momentum is high."

This isn't a feature. It's a different mental model for what software can be.

Most people are trying to make AI work harder in their existing workflows. The builders who get this are redesigning what a workflow even is.

Unpopular opinion: In 18 months, "prompt engineering" will be to AI agents what "writing cron jobs" is to modern DevOps. A useful primitive, but not where the thinking happens.

The thinking happens in loop design.
