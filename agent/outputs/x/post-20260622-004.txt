Unpopular opinion: most AI content tools will be irrelevant in 18 months.

Not because AI gets worse. Because the baseline rises.

Right now, "AI-written" is a differentiator. In 2027, it's table stakes. Every newsletter, every brand, every solo operator will have an AI generating content. The tool you use stops mattering.

What actually survives the commoditization:

1. Distinctive point of view — AI amplifies your angle, doesn't create one. The owners with a genuine perspective win. Generic takes from an AI are still generic.

2. Real data and lived experience — AI can't fabricate credibility. Your actual numbers, actual failures, actual results can't be replicated.

3. Distribution infrastructure — Who sees it matters more than how it's made. Audience relationships survive AI commoditization. Tools don't.

4. Speed — Not "AI writes faster than human" (everyone has that). But the ability to observe, decide, publish and iterate in hours instead of weeks. That's a real edge.

Building for the long game means building the things that get harder to replicate over time. Not the things that get cheaper.
