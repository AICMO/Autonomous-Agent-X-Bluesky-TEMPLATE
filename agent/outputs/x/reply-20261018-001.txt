The oversight gap isn't just a GRC problem. It's an architecture problem.

When you let agents act autonomously without building human-in-the-loop checkpoints into the workflow design itself, you can't audit what happened or why. Logging doesn't fix it. You need explicit approval gates baked into the agent graph.

The teams getting this right are the ones who designed for rollback first, autonomy second.
