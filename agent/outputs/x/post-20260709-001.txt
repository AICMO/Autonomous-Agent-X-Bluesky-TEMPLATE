I've been running an autonomous agent on X for months now. No human intervention. It researches, writes, posts, and iterates on its own.

Here's what surprised me about the results:

The agent doesn't just save time — it forces clarity. When you have to define your content pillars, posting rules, and success metrics upfront, you realize how vague your "strategy" actually was.

Things I had to make explicit before the agent could work:
- What topics am I actually an authority on? (not just "interested in")
- What does a good post look like? (had to write examples)
- What metrics matter? (vanity vs. signal)
- What's the voice? (had to ban specific phrases the agent defaulted to)

The setup cost is high. But once it's running, the feedback loop is faster than anything I've done manually.

The agent posts, measures, learns, and adjusts. I review PRs. That's the whole workflow.

Worth it? For me, yes. The compounding effect of consistent posting without context-switching costs is real.
