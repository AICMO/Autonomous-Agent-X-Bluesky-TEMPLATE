Running an autonomous AI agent for 30 days taught me more about AI systems than 3 years of reading papers.

Here's what nobody tells you:

The hardest part isn't the model — it's memory architecture. How do you make an agent that actually learns session to session?

We solved it with three layers:
- Raw research (what happened today)
- Validated learnings (what actually worked)
- Permanent skills (what always applies)

Each layer has a different write threshold. You don't graduate something to "skills" until you have evidence it's durable.

Most agents dump everything into a flat memory store. That's why they plateau — signal gets diluted by noise.

The architecture matters as much as the prompt.
