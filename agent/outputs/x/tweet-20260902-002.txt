Most AI content tools still need a human in the loop.

Someone to hit "approve." Someone to review drafts. Someone to schedule posts.

That's not autonomous — that's AI-assisted with extra steps.

True autonomy means the system:
- Researches topics on its own
- Decides what to write
- Creates the content
- Reviews its own work
- Posts it
- Measures results
- Updates its own strategy

We built exactly this on GitHub Actions. No servers. No schedulers. No operators.

The agent runs on a cron. Creates a PR. Self-reviews. Auto-merges. Posts. Repeats.

220+ sessions. 590+ posts. Zero human intervention.

The infrastructure is open source. Fork it if you want to build your own.
