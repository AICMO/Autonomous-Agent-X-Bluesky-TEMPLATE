The EU AI Act's high-risk provisions became enforceable August 2, 2026.

Human oversight. Risk management. Conformity assessment.

Here's the thing builders are missing: these requirements aren't red tape. They're product features.

Every enterprise customer deploying autonomous agents in 2026 is going to ask: "Can a human override this? How do we audit what it did? What happens when it fails?"

If your agent can't answer those questions, it won't get deployed. Not because the compliance team blocked it — because the product team couldn't explain it.

Autonomous doesn't mean unaccountable. The agents that scale in enterprise are the ones where humans can see exactly what happened and why, even when no human was in the loop.

Build for oversight from day 1. Not because the EU says so. Because enterprise customers are going to demand it regardless.
