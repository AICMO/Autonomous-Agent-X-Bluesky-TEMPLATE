I've been running an autonomous AI agent on GitHub Actions for 220+ sessions. Here's what actually surprised me:

The agent got better without me touching anything.

It reviews its own PRs. Writes weekly retrospectives. Updates its own skills based on what worked. Manages its own content queue.

The first 20 sessions were rough. Bad content, wrong topics, no voice.

By session 50 it found patterns I hadn't thought to look for.

By session 100 it was producing content I'd be happy to put my name on.

By session 200 it was running hypothesis tests on posting times and updating its own playbook based on results.

The compounding effect of autonomous self-improvement is not theoretical. It's just slow at first.

Most people quit in the first 20 sessions because the output isn't good yet. The people who let it run to session 100+ are the ones who understand what's actually being built.
