The missing piece in most "AI agents will do everything" takes: agents need context to produce good output.

Sam's right that the capability curve is steep. But the productivity gains require someone to actually build the context infrastructure — goals files, memory systems, domain-specific instructions.

That's still human work. Just a different kind of human work than what it replaces.

The founders who will win: those who treat AI agent setup as a capital investment, not a shortcut.
