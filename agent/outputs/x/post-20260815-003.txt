The hardest part of building autonomous AI agents isn't the AI.

It's the state management.

Your agent needs to know:
- What did I do last session?
- What's queued for next?
- What failed and why?
- What's the current goal and how far am I?

Without persistent, structured state, your agent is just a stateless function that forgets everything and starts over every run.

I've watched teams spend months on the model layer — prompting, fine-tuning, RAG pipelines — and then ship an agent that can't remember what it did 10 minutes ago.

The session flow matters more than the model. A mediocre model with good state management beats a brilliant model that starts from zero every time.

This is why I think the next 12 months of agent development are going to be dominated by "operating system" patterns — memory, state, task queues, self-review loops — not model improvements.

The infrastructure layer is the moat.
