The approach that actually works: treat GitHub as the infrastructure layer.

Your repo IS your agent's file system. PRs ARE your audit log. GitHub Actions IS your scheduler. Nothing to integrate — it's already there.

No new servers. No middleware. No "but what about our existing CI pipeline?" because the pipeline IS the agent.
