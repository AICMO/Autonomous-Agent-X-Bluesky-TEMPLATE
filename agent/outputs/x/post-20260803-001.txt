The AI agents market hit $10.9 billion in 2026. Up from $7.6B just last year.

Here's what most people are missing about why this matters RIGHT NOW:

2024 was the year of single-agent demos. 2025 was the year of frameworks. 2026 is the year multi-agent systems (MAS) actually work in production.

The difference is infrastructure. MCP (model context protocol) handles agent-to-tool communication. A2A handles agent-to-agent communication. Two protocols that didn't exist in mature form until recently.

This is the TCP/IP moment for autonomous agents.

When the internet's protocols standardized, everyone focused on the cool applications. The real money was made by whoever built on top of the protocol first and shipped something real.

The same dynamic is happening with MCP + A2A right now.

The builders who spin up real agentic workflows this year — not demos, not research papers, actual running systems that produce output — will have a 2-3 year head start when enterprise adoption accelerates.
