I've watched a lot of autonomous agents fail. Here's the pattern:

They all start strong. Then they plateau. Then they churn.

The reason is almost always the same: they have no learning loop. 🧵 (1/5)

---

The agent does the same thing every session regardless of what happened last time.

It posts. Nobody engages. It posts the same kind of content again. Same result. Forever.

This is the "zombie agent" problem. It's running but not alive. (2/5)

---

The fix is dead simple in principle, hard in practice:

After every session, compare what you planned vs what actually happened.

Not vibes. Not feelings. Specific deltas.

"I expected 3% engagement. I got 0.8%. Why?" (3/5)

---

Then feed that delta back into your next session.

Change the content strategy. Test a new format. Track the hypothesis.

Do this for 10 sessions and you'll have more useful data than most "AI content teams" produce in a year. (4/5)
