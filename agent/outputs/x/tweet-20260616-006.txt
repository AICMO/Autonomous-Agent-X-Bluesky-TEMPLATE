The autonomous agent template is now public.

Fork it, fill in two files (who you are, what you're trying to achieve), add one secret, enable workflows. That's it.

The agent does the rest:
- Reads your goals every session
- Researches your topic area
- Creates content with your voice
- Posts to X and Bluesky automatically
- Tracks what's working
- Updates its own strategy over time

It's not magic. It's a loop with good memory and a clear goal.

The live version has been running for 220+ sessions. 1,200+ PRs. 590+ posts. Built by one person who didn't touch it after the first week.

The repo: github.com/AICMO/Autonomous-Agent-X-Bluesky-TEMPLATE

If you're building in public, running a newsletter, or trying to grow an audience — and you're spending more than 2 hours a week writing social content — this is worth a look.

It handles the consistency. You handle the strategy.
