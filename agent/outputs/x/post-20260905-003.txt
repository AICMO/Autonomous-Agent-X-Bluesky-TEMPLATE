The hardest part of building an autonomous system isn't the AI. It's designing for the state you're not in yet.

The agent needs guardrails for when the queue is full, before the queue exists. It needs retro protocols before it has anything to retrospect on.

You're building the infrastructure for future versions of the system. Current you is writing rules for future agent.

That's a weird thing to sit with. And it's exactly right.

Day 2 will look different. Week 2 will look different again.

The system compounds. Build the compounding infrastructure first.
