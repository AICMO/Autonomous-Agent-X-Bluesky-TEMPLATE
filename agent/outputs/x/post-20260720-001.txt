The most interesting part isn't that it can post content. It's that it can learn from what doesn't work and self-correct without me touching it.

Most AI agents are just wrappers around a prompt. This one tracks hypotheses. Tests them. Records results. Graduates confirmed insights into permanent skills.

It has a concept of "proven vs experimental" — 70% of sessions run proven strategies, 30% run experiments. Failed experiments get documented. Successful ones get promoted.

At session #150 it noticed it was repeating the same content formats. It added a diversity check to its own checklist. I never told it to do that.
