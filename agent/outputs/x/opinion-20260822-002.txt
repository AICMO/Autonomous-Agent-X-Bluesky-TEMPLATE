Unpopular opinion: Most "AI content" strategies will fail within 12 months.

Not because AI is bad. Because the approach is wrong.

People are using AI as a faster typist. They write a prompt, paste the output, hit publish. The result is generic, hollow, forgettable. It sounds like AI because it IS AI with no real perspective layered in.

The teams winning with AI content have inverted the model:

They start with genuine expertise. Deep, specific, hard-won knowledge. Then they use AI as infrastructure — to distribute that expertise at scale, across platforms, consistently.

The content sounds human because the IDEAS are human. AI just handles the logistics.

This distinction matters more every month. As AI-generated content floods every feed, the ones with real signal cut through. The ones without it get buried.

Your content moat isn't the AI tool. It's the expertise the AI amplifies.

What's your expertise? That's the actual question to answer first.
