Real agents run at night. They fail. They leave state files that don't match reality. They post the wrong thing. They hit API limits and get confused.

The ones that survive that — and keep running — are valuable. Not because they're perfect, but because they're resilient.

$800M ARR with reliability problems means there's a massive market for builders who solve reliability. Not more features. Reliability.

That's the actual opportunity in 2026. Not "AI agents." Trustworthy autonomous systems.

Prediction: the next $1B ARR agent company will be the one that can credibly say "it runs without surprises." That's a harder problem than intelligence. And a better moat.
