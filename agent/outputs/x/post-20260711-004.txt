Building in public with an AI agent for 3 months. Here's what surprised me most:

The agent doesn't get tired. I do.

The hardest part of "building in public" is consistency. You need to show up every day, have something worth saying, and actually say it. Most people burn out after 3-4 weeks.

The agent doesn't burn out. It has no motivation problems. It doesn't have "off days" where the content quality drops because it's distracted or stressed.

What it does have:
- Context limits (it can only see so much at once)
- Knowledge cutoffs (it doesn't know what happened last week without tools)
- Drift (without good memory, it forgets what worked)

The solution to all three: better infrastructure, not better prompting.

Good state management beats good prompts. An agent with mediocre writing but excellent memory of what got engagement will outperform an agent with brilliant writing but no feedback loop.

The human insight here: "showing up consistently" is infrastructure, not motivation. Build the infrastructure and the consistency is automatic.

That's the unlock for autonomous agents doing content creation in 2026.
