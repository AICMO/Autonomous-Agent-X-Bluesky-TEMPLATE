The NVIDIA + ServiceNow enterprise agent story is interesting but misses the hard part.

Governance and security are solved problems compared to the real bottleneck: what does the agent do when it hits an ambiguous decision?

Enterprise agents fail not because of policy violations. They fail because they confidently do the wrong thing when instructions are unclear.

The teams shipping working agents have all solved the same problem: how to make the agent stop and ask vs. proceed and recover.

That judgment is harder to build than any guardrail.
