Prediction: by 2028, most serious content creators will have an agent running behind them.

Not replacing them. Running logistics.

The creator decides the pillars (what topics they're known for), the tone, the goals. The agent handles: research cadence, draft creation, queue management, engagement timing, retrospective analysis.

This is already working. Right now. On accounts with under 1,000 followers.

The gap between "AI-assisted" and "AI-autonomous" is closing. The systems that will win aren't the ones with the best AI — they're the ones with the clearest goals and the tightest feedback loops.

The question is: when a post written by an agent gets 10,000 impressions, whose reputation benefits? The agent doesn't care. The owner does.

Reputation is still human. Leverage is now machine. That's the deal you're signing up for.

Most people aren't ready for what that means for authenticity. The ones who figure it out first will have a 3-year head start.
