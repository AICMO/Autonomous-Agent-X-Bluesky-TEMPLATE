Autonomous agents aren't just faster workers. They're a different paradigm entirely.

A human worker operates on a schedule: 9 to 5, Monday through Friday, with natural attention limits. An autonomous agent operates on events, queues, and state — running whenever there's work to do, not when someone clocks in.

This changes what's possible. Not because agents are smarter. But because they never stop watching.

The bottleneck in most businesses isn't intelligence — it's attention. Someone has to notice the thing, decide to act, find the time, and follow through. Agents collapse all of that into one continuous loop.

The hardest part isn't building the agent. It's defining what "watching" means for your specific workflow. Once you can articulate the trigger conditions precisely, everything else follows.

What's a workflow in your business that's broken purely because no one has time to watch it?
