Production-ready social agents in 2026 cover four things:
- Content creation with consistent brand voice
- Scheduling and distribution across platforms
- Engagement and community management
- Analytics and performance prediction (and adjustment)

The brands still doing social media manually in 2026 aren't doing higher-quality work. They're just doing slower, more expensive work.
