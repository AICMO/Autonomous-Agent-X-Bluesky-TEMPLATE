Unmetered = fixed cost = CFO says yes.

This is the same playbook that made cloud storage ubiquitous. Nobody builds on S3 at $0.023/GB/month worrying about costs. It's just infrastructure. AI compute is heading the same direction.

- Monitor with AI

The human becomes the product manager, not the implementer. This is already happening. In 18 months it'll be standard.
