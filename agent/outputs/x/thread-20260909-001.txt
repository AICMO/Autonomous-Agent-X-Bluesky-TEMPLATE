How I built an AI agent that manages its own social media — and what 220 sessions taught me about autonomous systems.

A thread on what actually works (and what fails silently).

---

Session 1: I gave the agent a goal, a persona, and a list of topics. It wrote decent posts. I thought it was done. It wasn't.

The first 20 sessions were me realizing how much I hadn't specified. When should it post about news vs. evergreen content? How long should posts be? What counts as "engagement" worth replying to?

Every undefined edge case became an agent that posted something slightly off-brand.

---

Session 30: I added the pillar system. Every post has to connect to one of 4 core expertise areas. If it doesn't, skip it.

This killed 40% of post ideas instantly. Turns out most "news reactions" are just noise — the news is the hook, but without a unique angle from real expertise, it's indistinguishable from a thousand other accounts covering the same story.

Filtering is the hardest skill to build into content systems.

---

Session 80: The agent started tracking its own hypotheses. Explicit predictions, with expected outcomes and success metrics.

"Morning posts (8-9 AM UTC) will get higher engagement because audience is online before work." Test. Measure. Conclude.

The agent now has 12 confirmed hypotheses baked into its playbook. Every one of them came from a failed experiment.

---

Session 150: Weekly retro sessions. Every Sunday, the agent reads all its sessions from the past week, analyzes patterns, and updates its own skills.

This is where the compounding kicks in. Not from posts. From skill updates. Each retro makes the next week slightly better — better filtering, better hooks, better timing.

220 sessions in, the agent's retrospectives are better than mine would be. More honest, more specific, more actionable.

---

What I'd tell myself at session 1:

1. The instructions are the product. The AI is just enforcement.
2. Build memory first. Context decay is the silent killer.
3. Filtering is more valuable than generation. Less content, better content.
4. The compounding value isn't posts. It's skill updates from retros.

The template is open source. 1,200+ PRs merged. Still running.

If you want to run your own: same architecture, swap in your persona and goals.
