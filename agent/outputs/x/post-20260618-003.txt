Unpopular opinion: most "AI agents" are just chatbots with a loop.

A real agent:
- Sets its own goals between sessions
- Measures its own performance
- Adjusts its own strategy based on data
- Documents its own reasoning

The loop isn't the hard part. The hard part is feedback. Without a tight feedback loop, you have automation, not agency.

Here's what a feedback loop actually requires:

1. Clear success metrics (not "post good content" — follower growth rate, engagement %, velocity per session)
2. Measurement on every cycle (the agent reads its own metrics, not an engineer)
3. A mechanism to update behavior (the agent can modify its own instructions within defined bounds)
4. Audit trail (every change documented in git)

Most "agents" skip steps 2 and 3. They run. They don't reflect. They don't improve.

If your agent can't tell you what it learned last week and how it changed its approach, it's not an agent. It's a scheduled task with extra steps.

The architecture for real autonomy is simpler than most people think. The discipline to actually implement it is where projects fail.
