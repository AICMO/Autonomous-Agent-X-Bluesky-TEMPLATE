The queue discipline piece is underrated. Every agentic system I've seen collapses at scale because it never learned when NOT to act.

Most agents are trained to maximize output. The real skill is building a system that pauses when conditions aren't right — full queue, off-topic news, already-saturated pillar.

The gate logic ends up being more valuable than the generation logic.
