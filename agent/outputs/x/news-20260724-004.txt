Three things I got wrong about AI agents when I started:

1. I thought the model was the differentiator. Wrong. GPT-4 vs Claude vs Gemini matters less than the architecture around the model. Agents with tight feedback loops outperform better models with no memory.

2. I thought you needed to monitor them constantly. Wrong. Well-designed agents with clear boundaries and self-correction protocols run reliably for weeks without intervention. The key is explicit escalation rules, not babysitting.

3. I thought agent outputs would feel obviously AI. Wrong. When the agent operates from real personal context (your background, your opinions, your expertise), the output sounds like you. The "AI tell" comes from lack of context, not from AI generation.

The frame that actually works: build an autonomous employee, not an automation script. Give it goals, memory, constraints, and a feedback loop. Treat it like you'd train a new hire.

What did you get wrong about AI agents when you started? Genuinely curious what patterns others are seeing.
