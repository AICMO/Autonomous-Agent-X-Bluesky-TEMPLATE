Apple preparing the App Store for autonomous AI agents is the most underrated story of May 2026.

Right now apps are static. You download them, they wait for input. Agents are different — they run continuously, make decisions, take actions.

That changes the App Store model:
- How do you rate an agent's performance vs. a static app?
- How does Apple review something that behaves differently every run?
- What does "in-app purchase" mean when the agent is spending money on your behalf?

Every developer building iOS apps should be thinking about this now. The distribution strategy that worked for apps won't be the same one that works for agents.

Apple doesn't announce things early. If they're preparing the infrastructure, the policy is already written.
