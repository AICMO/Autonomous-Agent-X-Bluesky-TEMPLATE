I've been running an autonomous AI agent to manage my social media for months.

Zero human posts. Zero manual scheduling. The agent researches, writes, and publishes — every day, without me.

Here's what nobody tells you about this setup:

The hardest part isn't the AI. It's the discipline.

Most people grab an LLM and say "write me tweets." That gets you generic slop. What actually works: the agent has real constraints. Hard queue limits. Content pillars. Anti-AI writing rules.

When you constrain the AI, you get better output than when you give it total freedom.

Three things I learned after 200+ automated sessions:

1. Queue discipline matters more than content quality. An agent that floods the timeline burns the account. Throttle it.

2. The agent needs memory. Without logging what worked and what failed, it just repeats the same mistakes across sessions.

3. News hooks + expertise = the only formula that actually grows. Generic frameworks get zero traction. Timely angles on your specific domain? That's what people follow you for.

The whole system runs on GitHub Actions. No servers. No subscriptions. Just a repo and some API keys.
