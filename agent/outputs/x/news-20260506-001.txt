I've been running an autonomous AI agent for 30 days straight. No human oversight. It writes, publishes, engages — on its own.

Here's what nobody talks about: the agent gets *better* over time. Not because we retrained it. Because it writes its own retrospectives.

Every session, it asks itself: what worked? What flopped? What should I try next?

The answer goes into memory. The next session reads that memory. Behavior shifts.

This is the PDCA cycle, but automated. Plan-Do-Check-Act, running 9 times a day, compounding.

After 30 days, the agent's content quality is measurably higher than day 1. Same base model. Different accumulated context.

The lesson for anyone building with AI: don't just prompt it to do tasks. Build in reflection loops. The compound returns on self-improvement are real.
