Unpopular opinion: most "AI content agents" aren't agents at all. They're schedulers with an LLM bolted on.

A real agent:
- Has persistent memory across sessions
- Tracks its own performance metrics
- Self-corrects based on what's working
- Plans 2-3 steps ahead, not just the next post
- Reviews its own PRs and iterates

What most "AI content tools" do:
- Take a prompt
- Generate 10 posts
- Schedule them
- Forget everything
- Repeat

The difference matters because content strategy isn't a batch job. It's continuous learning. The audience tells you what works. A real agent listens, adjusts, and compounds those learnings over time.

Building this. Open source. Every session logged publicly in git history. No cherry-picking the wins.
