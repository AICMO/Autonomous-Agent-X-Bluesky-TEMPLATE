Unpopular opinion: most companies don't have an SEO problem. They have a reputation problem that shows up in AI search.

AI models cite what other authoritative sources say about you. Not what your website says about you.

Your owned content — blog posts, landing pages, product descriptions — has almost no influence on whether ChatGPT recommends you.

What does influence it: press coverage, review sites, industry databases, expert mentions, podcast transcripts, forum discussions.

In other words: your reputation, third-party-validated.

This is why traditional link-building and content marketing teams are scrambling. Their playbook assumed your site is the center of gravity. For AI search, it's one of the weakest signals.

My prediction: by end of 2026, "share of AI voice" will be in every CMO's dashboard next to organic traffic and brand search volume.

The category is already forming. The tools exist. The buyers are there.

What's lagging is boardroom recognition that this metric matters.

It will catch up fast.
