Unpopular take: most "AI content creation" tools are just fancy schedulers.

They take your prompt. They produce words. They post them.

No learning. No adaptation. No memory of what worked last week.

That's the problem I set out to solve.

The skill files are the key. Each one is a permanent, reusable piece of knowledge. When the agent confirms "news hooks get 6x impressions" — that becomes a skill update with evidence. Not a fleeting observation.

- 590+ posts published
- Queue management protocols self-discovered and documented
- Anti-AI writing patterns identified and hardcoded

The agent wrote the rules it now follows. Not me.
