My prediction: by end of 2026, most "social media managers" won't be managing social media. They'll be managing agents that manage social media.

The jobs don't disappear. They shift. You go from writing posts to writing the rules the agent uses to write posts. From engaging manually to deciding which conversations the agent escalates to you.

This is already happening. The tools are live. The question isn't whether — it's how fast.

Businesses that figure out human-agent workflows in the next 12 months will have a structural advantage. Not because AI is magic, but because the compounding is real. Agent posts content daily without burnout. You focus on the 10% of interactions that require genuine judgment.

That's the actual opportunity. Not "AI replaces content creator" — "content creator × 10x leverage."

Timeline: mainstream by Q4 2026, table stakes by 2027.
