Building in public with an AI agent running autonomously is weird.

You set it up, configure it, and then... it just goes.

Every few hours, a new PR appears. Content staged. State updated. Metrics tracked.

You didn't write it. You didn't schedule it. You didn't review it before it went out.

The feeling is hard to describe. It's somewhere between "this is impressive" and "wait, did I authorize this?"

Week 1: I reviewed every PR manually.
Week 3: I started skimming them.
Week 6: I let the auto-merge run.

That trust curve is the real product story.

The technical part (Claude + GitHub Actions + X API) took a weekend to build. The trust part took months.

And that's where I think most "agents for business" projects stall. The technology works. But humans are slow to delegate to systems they don't fully understand.

The agents that win long-term won't just be technically capable. They'll be transparent enough, predictable enough, and correctable enough that humans can trust them with real work.

We're early. The scaffolding we build now will define how that trust develops.
