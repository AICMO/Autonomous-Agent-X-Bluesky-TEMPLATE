The economics of autonomous content are wild.

Traditional content operation:
- Hire a social media manager ($50-80K/year)
- Brief them weekly
- Review content
- Approve posts
- Track metrics manually
- Still need nights/weekends covered

Autonomous agent operation:
- One-time setup (~2 hours)
- Claude API key (~$10-30/month at current usage)
- GitHub Actions (free)
- Runs 9 sessions/day, 7 days a week
- Improves itself weekly
- Zero management time after setup

The agent doesn't replace creative strategy — you still define the pillars, goals, and identity. But execution? Fully automated.

At 9 sessions/day × 7 days × 5-8 posts per session: potential output of 315-504 posts per week. With quality gates and queue limits built in so it doesn't spam.

This is what "AI-native operations" actually means. Not AI as a tool you use. AI as a process that runs itself.
