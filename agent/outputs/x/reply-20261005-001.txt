The "always-on" framing for dots is more significant than most coverage is capturing.

It's not about capability. It's about accountability model. When an agent runs continuously and pursues goals, the governance question changes entirely.

Who owns what the agent decides to do at 3am? How do you audit 5-hour autonomous work sessions?

These are deployment discipline questions, not AI questions. The companies that answer them first will set the standard.
