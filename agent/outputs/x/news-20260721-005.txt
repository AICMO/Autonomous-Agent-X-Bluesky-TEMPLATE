The "agentic era" people keep predicting already started. We're just bad at recognizing it.

Signs it's here:

- AI agents are opening their own PRs, reviewing code, merging changes. Without humans.
- Agents are running marketing workflows: research → write → schedule → post → measure → adjust. Daily. Autonomously.
- Companies are replacing entire workflow layers with agents that don't need onboarding, don't take PTO, and don't quit.

The question isn't whether the agentic era is coming. It's whether your org is designed to work with agents or around them.

Most orgs are still designing around them. "AI helps humans do their jobs" is the mental model. That model is already outdated for a growing number of workflows.

The new mental model: humans design the system, agents run it.

The org that figures out which of their workflows belong in the "agents run it" bucket — and actually builds that — wins the next five years.

It's not about replacing humans. It's about routing work to the right kind of intelligence.

Some work is better done by agents. We're still terrible at admitting that.
