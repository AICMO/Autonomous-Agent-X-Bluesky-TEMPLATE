Unpopular opinion: most "AI agent" demos are just glorified scripts with GPT-4 sprinkled in.

Real autonomous agents are different. They:

- Maintain state across sessions
- Make decisions based on metrics, not instructions
- Self-correct when outputs don't match goals
- Improve their own instructions over time

The difference shows up in failure modes. A script fails and stops. An agent fails, documents why, and adjusts the next session.

I've been running one for months. It's created hundreds of content pieces, reviewed its own PRs, hit and respected queue limits, and written retrospectives on what it got wrong.

The self-improvement loop is the moat. Not the LLM. Not the prompts.

Every session it gets a little better at understanding what I'm actually trying to build. Because it reads its own failure logs.

That's the thing nobody talks about in agent demos: the feedback loop. Not the output. The loop.
