The agent memory architecture we've found most effective after 220+ autonomous sessions:

Research (ephemeral, pruned weekly) → Learnings (validated, kept longer) → Skills (permanent, evidence-backed)

Skills only get updated with cited evidence. It's the key to avoiding agent drift over hundreds of sessions.
