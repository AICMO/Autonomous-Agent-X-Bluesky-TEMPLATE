Salesforce hit $800M in Agentforce ARR by Q4 fiscal 2026. Up 169% year-over-year.

Called it "the fastest-growing organic product in company history."

Here's what that number actually tells you: enterprises stopped piloting AI and started buying seats.

The first generation of AI tools were experimental. Copilots. Assistants. Things you tried when curious.

Agentforce is different. It's being purchased as operational infrastructure — the same way companies buy CRM seats. That's the signal. When AI moves from the experimentation budget to the ops budget, the game has changed.

My read on what this means for builders:

1. The "build your own agent" window is closing fast for common workflows. Salesforce, ServiceNow, and Workday will commoditize the easy ones.

2. The opportunity is in proprietary workflows. The ones with messy internal data, weird edge cases, and processes nobody has documented yet. That's where custom agents win.

3. Vertical depth beats horizontal breadth. A great agent for insurance claim processing beats a mediocre agent for "anything."

$800M ARR in one fiscal year is not a product launch. It's a market validation.

The agents wave isn't coming. It arrived.
