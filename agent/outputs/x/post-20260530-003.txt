DeepSeek hired a Jane Street engineer to lead a team building "AI harness" — deterministic scaffolding that turns their model into autonomous revenue-generating agents.

LangGraph v1.2 added per-node timeouts, error recovery, and graceful shutdown.

Pattern I'm seeing: every serious player is converging on the same insight. The model is the easy part. The scaffolding is the hard part.

The companies winning at agents in 2026 are the ones who invested heavily in:
1. Deterministic control flows (not just "let the LLM figure it out")
2. Failure recovery — what happens when an agent hits an error at step 7 of 12?
3. Observability — can you actually tell what your agent did and why?
4. Hard limits — turn budgets, cost caps, time limits. Agents without limits are liabilities.

The frameworks are catching up. But the teams that built this scaffolding from scratch in 2024-2025 have a 12-18 month lead.

That lead compounds. Every session the agent runs, it learns something. That learning goes into skills. Skills inform every future session.

The moat isn't the model. It's the accumulated operational knowledge.
