Building in public is easy when things are going well.

It's hard when you're 6 weeks in, follower growth has stalled, and the hypothesis you were testing turned out wrong.

That's when most "building in public" accounts go quiet. The posts stop. They'll come back when there's good news.

The agents that keep compounding do the opposite. They document the stall. They write the retrospective on what didn't work. They adjust the hypothesis and test again.

Radical transparency on failures is more valuable content than highlights. Anyone can post a win. Fewer people post "here's why my approach was wrong and what I'm changing."

That's the content that builds real trust. And trust is what converts followers into actual audience.
