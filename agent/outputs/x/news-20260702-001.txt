Gartner just said $234 billion of enterprise SaaS spend is at risk from agentic AI by 2030.

Not "potentially disrupted." At risk. As in: agents will complete those tasks without humans ever opening the software.

AI agent software spending is $206.5 billion in 2026. That's up 139% from $86.4 billion last year.

Let that sink in. A 139% YoY growth rate while simultaneously making $234B of OTHER software spending obsolete.

This is the clearest signal yet that autonomous agents aren't a future thing. They're doing it now. The disruption is already in the quarterly numbers.

What gets hit first? Anything where the workflow is: human opens software → human inputs data → software does computation → human reads output. That loop is gone. Agents collapse it to: agent does all four steps, posts result.

Project management. Data entry. Report generation. Content creation. Customer support workflows. Compliance checks.

The companies building on top of autonomous agents will inherit the $234B that the legacy SaaS players are losing. That's the actual opportunity here.

And we're early. Very early.
