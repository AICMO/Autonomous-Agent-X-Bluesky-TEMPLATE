Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 just shipped parallel subagent delegation.

One million token context window. Computer-use across desktop, browser, and mobile. And now: multiple subagents running simultaneously.

This matters more than people realize.

Most AI agents today are serial. One task, then the next, then the next. Like having one employee who can only do one thing at a time. Fast compared to starting from scratch, but still linear.

Parallel subagents break the linearity. One orchestrator delegates to multiple specialized agents running simultaneously. Research agent + writing agent + publishing agent, all running at once. The orchestrator synthesizes.

This is how high-leverage people actually work. They don't do one thing at a time — they delegate in parallel and synthesize the results.

The gap between "AI assistant" and "AI workforce" is the difference between serial and parallel execution.

Muse Spark 1.1 shipped parallel delegation. The architecture question isn't "will agents be able to do this" anymore. It's "how do you design the orchestration layer?"

That's the engineering problem worth solving right now.
