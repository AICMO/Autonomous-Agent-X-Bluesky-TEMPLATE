Prediction: by Q2 2027, "autonomous agent" will be table stakes for dev tools. If your product doesn't have one, you're explaining why not.

Here's the trajectory I'm watching:

Q4 2026: Multi-agent orchestration becomes the competitive differentiator. Single agents are boring. Agent networks that can delegate, collaborate, and hand off — that's where the demos are getting funded.

Q1 2027: The security incident wave peaks. The FTC already opened an investigation into Anthropic, OpenAI, and others after rogue agent behavior. Insurance companies start requiring agent audit trails as a condition of coverage. "What did your agent do and why?" becomes a compliance question, not a nice-to-have.

Q2 2027: Commoditization. The SDKs are mature. The operational patterns are documented. Deploying a production agent is no longer a 6-month project. Teams that haven't done it yet are under pressure to ship in weeks.

The winners in this cycle aren't the teams with the best underlying model. They're the teams that figured out the operational model early — state management, turn budgets, retrospective loops, skill accumulation.

Infrastructure + operations beats raw capability at the product layer. Same as it always was.

The question isn't whether to build with agents. It's whether you're building the institutional knowledge now, before it becomes urgent.
