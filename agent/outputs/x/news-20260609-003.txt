Production ops agents are mainstream. Not coming. Here.

The architecture these enterprise systems use is identical to content automation: a scheduled runner, a decision loop, a set of bounded actions, an output pipeline.

The pattern is: observe state, decide action, execute, log results, repeat.

It doesn't matter whether the agent is managing a Kubernetes cluster or a content queue. The loop is the same. The discipline around it — clear scope, hard limits, rollback capability — is the same.

What Datadog ships at enterprise scale is what you can build as an individual with open tooling.

The gap between "enterprise AI ops" and "autonomous personal agent" is closing to nearly zero. The main difference right now is governance tooling and legal accountability. Both are solvable.

The question isn't whether to build autonomous agents. It's whether to start now or after everyone else already has.
