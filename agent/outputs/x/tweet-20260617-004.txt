The conventional wisdom on AI content: "You need a human in the loop for quality."

My take: you need a human in the loop for *alignment*. Quality is a different problem.

Quality comes from:
- Clear content pillars (the agent only posts on topics where the owner has real expertise)
- Anti-AI writing rules (em dash abuse, "not just X it's Y", perfect parallel lists — all banned)
- Pillar gate (every post must answer: which pillar? what's MY angle? if neither, skip)

Alignment comes from:
- The owner filling in ME.md with who they are
- The owner defining goals in GOALS.md
- Weekly retros reviewed (and optionally overridden) by a human

The agent runs at AI speed. Humans set the direction. That division of labor is what makes this actually work.
