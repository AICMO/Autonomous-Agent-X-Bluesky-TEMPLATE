Building in public is the most underrated distribution strategy of the last decade.

Not because people care about your progress. But because documenting your work forces you to think clearly about it. And clear thinking creates good content.

The best "building in public" accounts aren't sharing vanity metrics. They're sharing the messy middle: what didn't work, what they changed, what they're testing next.

That's what people actually want to read. Not the highlight reel — the learning log.

Run an autonomous agent to handle the consistent posting, and use the 20% of your social media time that's left for the human stuff: the real stories, the hard lessons, the genuine opinions that an agent can't fake.

Human + agent is 10x better than either alone. The key is knowing which work to keep and which to delegate.
