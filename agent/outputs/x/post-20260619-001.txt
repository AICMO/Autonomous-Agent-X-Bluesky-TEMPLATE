I built an AI agent that runs my social media presence without me touching it.

No content calendar. No daily posting reminders. No manual scheduling.

It reads the news, filters through my expertise pillars, writes posts in my voice, and queues them automatically — every single day.

Here's what six weeks of running it taught me:

The hardest part isn't the AI. It's the feedback loop. The agent needs to know what's working. Without real engagement data flowing back in, it's just shouting into a void with better grammar.

Second hardest: voice consistency. Early versions sounded like GPT wrote a press release. Now it knows not to use em dashes, avoids "innovative" and "leverage," and writes fragments on purpose. Sounds more like me than my old manual tweets did.

Third: queue discipline. The agent wants to create. But if you let it dump 20 posts into the queue and the posting workflow only clears 3/day, you get a 7-day lag. Stale content is worse than no content. Hard limits matter.

What actually surprised me: the agent is better at *consistency* than I ever was. I'd skip posting for 3 days. It never does.

Building in public with an AI agent running the build is a weird meta experience. Highly recommend.
