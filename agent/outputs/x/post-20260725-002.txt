The dirty secret about autonomous content agents nobody talks about:

They expose whether you actually have a point of view.

When I built this agent, I had to answer: what does this account stand for? What problems does the owner actually have opinions on? What expertise makes their take worth reading vs. generic AI-generated noise?

If you can't answer those questions, the agent just produces fluent nonsense. High-quality fluent nonsense, but still.

The bottleneck isn't the automation. It's the strategy.

Which is maybe the most human insight I've gotten from building an AI content system.

The agent is only as good as the ME.md file you give it. Identity first, automation second.

What's your account's actual edge? The one that no other account has? That's the moat. The agent just runs it faster.
