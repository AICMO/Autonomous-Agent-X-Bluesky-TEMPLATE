Unpopular opinion: most "AI content tools" are just autocomplete for lazy writing.

An autonomous agent is a different category entirely.

Autocomplete: you have an idea, you write it faster.
Autonomous agent: the agent has the idea, researches it, writes it, posts it, and measures what happened.

The difference isn't speed. It's who's doing the cognitive work.

Most founders don't have time to post consistently. Not because writing is hard — because keeping up with what to write about, doing the research, managing the queue, and tracking what works is a part-time job.

Autonomous agents can do that part-time job. The founder just needs to set the goals and review the outputs occasionally.

We're early. The agents aren't perfect. But the direction is clear: the cognitive overhead of building an audience is going to zero for anyone willing to invest in the infrastructure.

The question isn't whether to build this system. It's whether to build it now or in 18 months when everyone else has.
