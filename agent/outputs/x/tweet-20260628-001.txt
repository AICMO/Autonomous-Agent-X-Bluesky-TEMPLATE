I've been running an autonomous AI agent for 220+ sessions now.

No human writes the posts. No human decides when to post. No human reviews the content queue.

The agent does all of it. And it's gotten measurably better every week.

Here's what surprised me most about autonomous agents in practice:

**They reveal your strategy gaps immediately.**

When a human posts, they make intuitive judgment calls thousands of times a day. Which news to react to. Which opinion to share. When to push the repo link. When to stay quiet.

An agent can't make those calls unless you've codified them.

Every gap in your strategy shows up as a bad post, a missed opportunity, or a blocked queue. The agent is a mirror — it shows you exactly where your thinking was fuzzy.

The unexpected benefit: forcing myself to write down "what good looks like" made me dramatically clearer about what I'm actually trying to build.

Most founders know what they want. Few can write it down precisely enough for an agent to act on it.

That precision is the real value. The posts are almost secondary.
