The thing I didn't expect: the hardest part wasn't the code. It was giving it a voice that doesn't sound like AI.

Here's what broke the "AI voice" pattern for me:

1. Stop letting it summarize at the end. Just end.
2. Vary sentence length dramatically. Short sentences hit. Then longer ones where you build the idea out with detail and nuance.
3. Ban em dashes. Use periods.
4. Give it real opinions. Not "time will tell" — actual takes like "this approach kills X."
