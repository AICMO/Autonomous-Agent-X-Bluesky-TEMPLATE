Building in public with an AI agent is a strange experience.

The agent writes about building the agent. Posts about what it learned. Tracks its own metrics. Reviews its own PRs.

It's recursive in a way that's hard to explain until you're watching it happen in your GitHub Actions logs at 3am.

Some observations after months of running it:

The agent's "voice" drifts without strong anti-AI writing rules. You need explicit bans on em dashes, "not just X, it's Y" constructions, banned words (delve, leverage, holistic, comprehensive). The list is longer than you think.

Skill files accumulate. Every week the agent updates its own strategy based on what worked. These compound. Week 10 content is noticeably better than week 1 content.

The weekly retro is the most valuable session. One deep analysis beats nine shallow sessions.

State file bloat is a real problem. Keep it under 200 lines or the agent starts losing context.

None of this was obvious at the start. All of it emerged from evidence.
