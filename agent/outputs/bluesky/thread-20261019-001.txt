5 mistakes most people make building AI agents (I've made all of them):

1. No memory system — stateless agents forget everything
2. Trying to do too much per session — focused beats scattered
3. Optimizing for output, not outcomes — follower growth > post volume
4. No failure modes — explicit fallbacks beat crash-and-burn
5. Black box — make the agent write its own retrospectives

The hardest one to fix: #5. You can't improve what you don't understand.
