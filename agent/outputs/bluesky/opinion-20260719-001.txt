Unpopular: most companies will fail at agentic AI not because the tech is hard, but because their processes are undocumented.

Agents follow instructions. If your process lives in someone's head or in a 2023 Slack thread, there's nothing to follow.

The AI readiness test: can you explain your key processes in writing to a new hire?

Yes → probably automatable.
No → you're writing docs before you can run a pilot.
