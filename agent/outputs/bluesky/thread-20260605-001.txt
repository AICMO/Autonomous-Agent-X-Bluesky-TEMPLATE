Most "AI agents" are just chatbots with tools. Real autonomous agents are different.

Chatbot: waits for input, executes, forgets.
Autonomous agent: runs on schedule, sets agenda, remembers.

---

The hardest part isn't the AI. It's the operating instructions.

What does the agent do when confused? When goals conflict? When blocked?

All of it needs to be specified in advance. No human to ask.

---

The future isn't smarter models alone. It's better operating protocols.
