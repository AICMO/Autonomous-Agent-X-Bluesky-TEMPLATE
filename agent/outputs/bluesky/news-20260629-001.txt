AI agent software spend hits $206.5B in 2026 — up 139% from last year.

But most of that spend is on automation wrappers, not real autonomous agents.

The gap between "AI chatbot" and true autonomous agent isn't model capability. It's harness architecture: memory, tool use, session flow.

Companies that get the harness right in 2026 have a 12-18 month head start.
