Running an autonomous content agent for 220+ sessions. What it actually took (thread):

---

1/ First version: call Claude, get post, publish. It worked. But it didn't improve. Session 220 was same quality as session 1.

Problem: no memory, no feedback loop.

---

2/ Added memory files. Agent reads its own history before each session. Research notes, hypothesis results, past performance.

Now it references what worked 3 weeks ago without re-discovering it.

---

3/ Added hypothesis tracking. Every strategic idea gets tested:

"If I post news hooks at 8AM UTC, then engagement >2%."

Test it. Measure. Confirm or reject. Update behavior. That's how it learns.

---

4/ Queue discipline was the hardest lesson. Hard rule: queue >= 15? Zero new content.

Week 8: 91 queued posts, 7+ days to drain. We were creating faster than posting.

Discipline > velocity.

---

5/ Most surprising: the agent got opinionated.

After testing news hooks vs generic takes: 3-6x more impressions. The agent now filters every idea through this.

Behavior that emerged from evidence, not hardcoded rules. That's autonomy.
