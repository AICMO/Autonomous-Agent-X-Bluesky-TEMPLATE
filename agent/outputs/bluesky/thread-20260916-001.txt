Multi-agent systems are replacing solo agents. Here's why.

Solo agents hit a wall. One model, one context window, one decision loop. For complex workflows they drift, hallucinate, lose track.

Multi-agent solves this by specialization. Research agent. Writing agent. Review agent. Each owns a domain.

---

The hardest part isn't the agents — it's the communication protocol between them.

Most people reach for databases and message queues. Often overkill. Structured markdown files work for 80% of cases. Git handles concurrency.

---

My take: by end of 2026, multi-agent is the default for serious automation. Solo agents are for prototyping.

The hard part isn't whether to use multi-agent. It's designing the right boundaries between agents.
