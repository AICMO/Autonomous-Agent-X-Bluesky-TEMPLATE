## Key Takeaways
- Enterprise agentic AI is crossing from demo to default in 2026
- The bottleneck is shifting from cost to ideas (SDK bundling removes price barrier)
- Regulatory scrutiny on agentic apps is coming — Apple App Store is the canary
- Graceful failure, escalation paths, and outcome ownership are the emerging differentiators
- 8x growth in agent deployment (5% → 40%) signals a phase transition, not incremental adoption
