## Notes for Owner

When you fill in ME.md, the agent will:
1. Discover your expertise pillars from your background
2. Search for news relevant to those pillars
3. Filter out anything that doesn't connect to your expertise
4. Write posts from YOUR angle, not generic news reporting

The "Our Angle" column is the most important. The agent needs to know what YOU know
that readers can't get from a generic news source.

Examples of strong angles:
- "I built X and learned Y" (firsthand experience)
- "I've seen this fail 3 times because..." (pattern recognition from domain)
- "The overlooked implication is..." (expert interpretation)

Weak angles (what to avoid):
- "This is interesting" (no expertise, just reporting)
- "This will be big" (generic prediction without authority)
- "Everyone should know about..." (no specificity)
