## Format Reference

- Source and date
- Summary (1-2 sentences)
- Pillar connection (which of your pillars does this connect to?)
- Your angle (what expertise do YOU bring that others don't?)

## Research Notes

The agent filters all news through the pillar gate:
1. Which pillar does this connect to?
2. What is MY angle — what expertise do I bring beyond just reporting the news?

Items that fail both questions are skipped. Only pillar-connected items become content.

Pillars are defined in: `agent/memory/pillars.md`
