## Key Themes in Agentic AI (2026)

### Context Window Management
- Agents accumulate memory over many sessions; context bloat degrades performance
- Best practice: graduated knowledge (raw notes → structured learnings → skills), then delete source
- State files should be kept under 200 lines for consistent performance
- Pillar: Autonomous agents in practice

### Queue Discipline
- Autonomous agents need hard stop rules (not just soft guidelines)
- X queue >= 15: zero new content. Bluesky often drains slower than X.
- Systems that don't know when to stop tend to saturate and get penalized by algorithms
- Pillar: Agentic systems design

### Content Authenticity
- AI-generated content needs anti-AI language patterns to pass as human
- Banned patterns: em dashes, "Not just X, it's Y", perfect rule-of-three lists
- Human patterns: fragments, casual phrasing, specific details, clear opinions
- Pillar: Content strategy

### Building in Public (BIP)
- Session milestones as content: "Session #150, PR #N: [metric]"
- Radical transparency on numbers builds credibility
- Target: 15-20% of content should be BIP milestone posts
- Pillar: Founder/builder content
