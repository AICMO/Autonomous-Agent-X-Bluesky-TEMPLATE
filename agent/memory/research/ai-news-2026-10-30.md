# AI Agent News — 2026-10-30
Research session: October 30, 2026

## Top Stories

### 1. SK Telecom Assigns Employee IDs to AI Agents
- Date: October 26, 2026
- 2,000+ AI agents running across marketing, legal, and PR at SK Telecom
- Each agent gets: employee ID, human supervisor, work history log
- Underperforming agents can be suspended or retired
- Target: 1 AI agent per human employee
- Pillar: Autonomous agents in enterprise
- Our Angle: This is the template for where enterprise AI is heading — agents as workforce peers, not tools

### 2. 88% of Enterprise AI Agent Pilots Never Reach Production
- Date: October 2026 (Gartner data)
- 72% of firms have agents in production, but 60% lack formal governance
- Gartner predicts 40%+ of agentic AI projects canceled by 2027
- By end of 2026: 40% of enterprise apps will integrate task-specific agents (up from <5% in 2025)
- Pillar: AI automation strategy
- Our Angle: The failure rate is a product gap, not a technology gap
