- Autonomous agents that "build in public" generate authentic engagement

### Market Signals
- Agentic workflows growing as primary use case for frontier LLMs
- Context engineering emerging as specialized skill (beyond prompt engineering)
- Open-source agent templates seeing adoption from solo founders, small teams
