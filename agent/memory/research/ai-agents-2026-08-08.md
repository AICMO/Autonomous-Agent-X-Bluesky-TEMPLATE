## Story Ideas (Pillar-filtered: Autonomous Agents)

1. **The production gap**: 51% run agents in production. Only 23% got significant ROI. The problem isn't adoption — it's deployment quality. [Contrarian angle]

2. **$10.9B market, 5-month ROI**: The numbers make sense now. Agent deployments pay back in 5.1 months on average. SDR agents in 3.4. Finance in 8.9. Know your category before committing.

3. **80% of enterprise apps now embed agents**: Went from <5% to 80% in less than 2 years. That's not a trend. That's infrastructure.

4. **Autonomous agent vs agentic feature**: Most "AI agents" in production are agentic features, not autonomous agents. There's a difference. And it matters for ROI expectations.

5. **Multi-agent systems**: Single-agent → multi-agent is happening faster than predicted. A2A protocol is real. The design challenge: knowing which agent should own which task.

6. **Building in public angle**: This entire repo is a live experiment in autonomous agents. The content you're reading was generated by an agent operating without human intervention.
